  `bnrose_saw_harness::saw_attempt == attempt8` for EVERY u8; and
  `bnrose_saw_harness::saw_admissible == gateAt` for EVERY flag pair.
  `attempt8` extends the twin's `attemptAfter` to the full u8 domain
  (0..14 → +1; 15..254 → 15; 255 → 0), and `attempt8Agrees` (Q.E.D.)

## 2. Saturation and replacement authority (Cryptol, BnroseComposition.cry)

The reuse of encoded slot 15 gets its own authorization argument: the
original attempt and the replacement carry the SAME operation identity
(identical encoding ⇒ identical op id by step_op_id's construction), so the
destination holds them to one effect — `saturationReuseSingleEffect`
(Q.E.D.: two dispatches of one id, at most one effect). A reconciled row
closes the gate on every node (`reconciledGateCloses`, Q.E.D. — the
durable-history condition; `oldRecordsStand` from the twin carries the
monotone ledger). The reuse cannot resurrect exhausted authority through a
recorded effect — `gateNeedsNeverExecuted` (#424) — and cannot create an
additional effect — the composition obligations here.

## 3. Cross-node composition (the destination assumption, explicit)

Two independent sovereign nodes may dispatch an identical authorized
intent: `twoDispatchesAtMostOneEffect` and `oneEffectWhenFreshAndDispatched`
(Q.E.D.) — at most one effect, exactly one when fresh and dispatched; the
partition shape is named and covered (`staleViewsStillOneEffect`: both
views stale, both gates open, both dispatch — still one effect). The
destination's op-keyed exactly-once apply is the model's shape
(`applyIdempotent`) and **an explicit assumption until a real Arbitrum or
Hive adapter is verified** — a locally generated operation id alone cannot
guarantee external idempotency. The load-bearing control: a NAIVE
destination without dedup REFUTES (`naiveDestinationDuplicates` —
counterexample `False True True`) — the enforcement is necessary, not
decorative.
