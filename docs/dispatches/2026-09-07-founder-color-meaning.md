# Founder color meaning — original logo handoff

On 2026-09-07 the founder supplied six original logo files and identified their
creators as himself and his mother. The original bytes are preserved in
`docs/mvp-walk/assets/genesis-3d/source/originals/` with their supplied filenames.

The founder stated:

> color never travels alone in BNR. purples communicate humans; teal is AI; greens are biomass

This statement accompanies the original artwork and the Blender study. The
study preserves the source gradients where these meanings meet. It does not
assign organizations to New bee, Raver or Cypherpunk, or treat these colors as
interchangeable decorative skins. The three reading views continue to describe
ways of using the same organism.

The 3D depth, neutral sidewalls and studio lighting are Astra's proposed
interpretation. They are not part of the supplied originals and are not a new
claim about authorship of those originals. The campaign seat was notified in
[PR #28](https://github.com/beehive-nature/beehive-nature/pull/28#issuecomment-5575660394)
that the missing-original assumption can now be retired.
