## Q2 — the PCC wills: pinned, plus three transcripts actually read

- **Jonathan Trevisa's will (the Q2 target):** TNA Discovery pins it exactly — *Will of Jonathan Trevisa, Merchant of London*, PCC register **Coke** (quires 1–56 — Vivian's "Coke 48"), proven **21 May 1669**, **PROB 11/329/575**. Same man documented in **East India Company correspondence, 1659** (BL IOR/E/3/26 ff. 71–72). Full text: TNA/Ancestry download only (paid) — the read remains gated.
- **BONUS — three PCC will transcripts retrieved free via Cornwall OPC:**
  1. **William Trevisa (the Major) will, 6 Jul 1673, PROB 11/342/690** — short nuncupative-style will: sons John and William only. **No sister Margaret, no Randells.**
  2. **William Trevisa (of Crocadon, the Major's son) will, 1680** — wife Mary, brothers Peter and Thomas, Bridget dtr of the late brother Richard, Manaton brothers-in-law. **No Randells.**
  3. **Mary Trevisa's will, 2 Jan 1680** (relict of the above William) — and here the new datum:
     > **"I give unto my Cosen Judith Manaton and my Cosen Margarett Randall and her sister liveing att the Royal Exchange twenty shillings a peice to be bestowed in Rings."**

## What the new primary datum changes

- **A "Margarett Randall" of the Trevisa kin was ALIVE 2 January 1680, in London, at the Royal Exchange, with a sister.** Near-certainly the widow of Rev. Mathew Randell (only Trevisa-Randall marriage in the family; "cosen" = the standard aunt-degree usage; Mary was Mary Manaton, wife of Margaret's nephew). This **contradicts the corpus's death-1668 for the mother-node** (FS "died 1668 High Bickington") — she lived past 1668, called only "Margarett Randall," still a married-name widow, living in the **London merchant world** (Royal Exchange) — the same milieu as the Bath-Randall textile merchants and the American John's own silk/textile trade, NOT the Cornish-gentry world.
- **No children of hers are named** in any of the three wills — but token ring-bequests to elderly cousins routinely skip remote children; not probative either way.
- The wills **neither authenticate nor contradict the parent-child edge directly.** They harden the environment (real marriage, real widow status, real London presence) while the specific son remains unrecorded everywhere.
