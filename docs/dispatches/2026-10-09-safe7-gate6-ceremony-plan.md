# 2026-10-09 — Safe 7 gate-6 ceremony plan (handoff §5 step 5; authorizes nothing)

## 0 · For the founder: one page

**What this will be.** A session, later, where you are present while a separate development Safe 7, never the one you use, runs for the first time a hardware image that carries the bpq app, and the unit's own post-quantum card, binding and file signature are checked against two oracles that are not the device, while every refusal the emulator already showed is shown again on glass. It is the audit's gate 6. This page asks you for nothing now: the software side continues on its own, and the day is named only after the readiness receipts exist.

**What it costs the development unit.** Unlocking its bootloader erases its seed and permanently makes its factory attestation key inaccessible; reinstalling official firmware does not bring that back, and Suite keeps warning that the device is not authentic from then on (the Trezor pages you relayed on 2026-10-09). A check taken before the unlock is a historical record of the unit as it was, not continuing attestation of the modified unit. The unit is bought for this, carries a public test identity only, is wiped at the end, and never holds value, a real recovery seed or the bzDiD phrase.

**What your hands will do.** Put the unit in bootloader mode; press Unlock on its red screen; compare the fingerprint under the install screen's info button with the one your agent names, then tap the checkmark, or the cross; tap through the red UNSAFE screen on every boot; read the six-digit pairing code off the screen so your agent can type it; enter the twelve public fixture words on the unit's own screen; read each confirmation page and hold to confirm, or cancel when the plan says cancel; say yes or no to what you see. Your agent runs every host step from scripts reviewed before the day; the unlock and the install are sent by those scripts and confirmed by your finger.

**What you never do.** Type a command. Type a seed word into any computer. Put the bzDiD phrase or a real recovery seed on the unit. Import anything from the unit into a browser or wallet page. Put any value on it. Hand a seed, passphrase or PIN to any agent. Touch your working Safe 7.

**What it costs in time.** About one power-on hour per leg: a watchdog the bootloader arms for this image resets the unit about an hour after each boot into the firmware. Three legs at most (pairing, seed and card; signatures and refusals; recovery and end), each resumable after a reset. The cable's power stays connected throughout. One optional ten-minute test (leaving a confirm screen alone until the unit locks itself) is settled on the day, not now.

**When the day comes.** After the reviewed build candidate exists with its reproducible-build receipt, the emulator cross-check at that same revision passes, the unit can be reached from the scripts' own shell, the earlier audit gates have their owners' receipts, and the privacy-safe receipt procedure is in place. None of that readiness authorizes an unlock, a flash, a wipe or a physical acceptance. The irreversible act happens only on your specific informed consent, asked then, with the consequence above in front of you.

## 4 · Abort rules

Each: stop; record the act number, the command, the unedited output into the founder-held local log, and into the public `aborts[]` entry the same output with any `Using path:` line removed and `redacted: ["path"]` declared (`python/src/trezorlib/cli/__init__.py:519-521`), and what the founder saw; no retry without a new receipt that names what changed.
- **Digest or fingerprint mismatch** at act 0 or 4: the file is not the named image; nothing is sent.
- **Oracle disagreement** (any check false in either oracle, or a check present in both oracles with different results in the two): the card or signature is not accepted; acts after it do not run.
- **A refusal that does not fire** (act 11, or a control at act 10): the device or an oracle accepted what it must refuse; the ceremony ends and the receipt says so.
- **A pairing prompt the founder cannot answer from the screen** (no code shown, or a prompt outside acts 1, 5 and 12): stop; record it; the keyring question in §8 is the first suspect.
- **A `WordRequest` reaching the host** at act 6 or 12: stop; no word is ever typed on a computer.
- **Watchdog reset mid-act**: the act is marked interrupted, not failed; resume per act 13, once; a second reset inside the same act is an abort.
- **The founder says stop**: stop at once, record the act and his word, wipe only if he says so.

## 6 · What this ceremony establishes, and does not

- **Exercised on hardware**, never "proved": the wording ceiling is "sound by construction / isolated by design" (CLAUDE.md §2 law 4; handoff `:128-129`; SPEC-BPQ-1 `:294-299`).
- **Fit numbers.** Signing time is measured for the first time (host-side round trip). On-device stack headroom and heap fragmentation have no readout on a non-debug image: the ceremony establishes only that N signatures returned without the allocation error the C path reports ("returns an error, not a crash", 10-05 receipt `hardware_fit.heap`); a readout is instrumentation nobody has designed and this plan does not design (ORDERS-1 `:61`).
- **No channel confidentiality.** The host link is classical Noise_XX THP (audit `:45`; SPEC-BPQ-1 `:297-298`): a device signature says who signed; it does not make the cable post-quantum.
- **Attestation lost, not gained.** Act 3 ends it for that unit, permanently: the factory attestation key becomes inaccessible, official firmware does not restore it, Suite keeps a non-authentic-device warning (the founder's relayed trezor.io sources, §1). A pre-unlock check (act 1) is historical evidence of the unit as it was and stays local; nothing here is device attestation (handoff `:129`).
- **A cross-check between implementations, not an audit** (`docs/bpq-device.md:100-102`; SPEC-BPQ-1 `:270-272`). Matching development images do not authorize installation (reproducible-build `:94-97`); only the founder's informed consent (P6) and his checkmark at act 4, on the day, will.
