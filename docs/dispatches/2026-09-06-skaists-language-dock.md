## Screenshot findings and changes

The clipped launcher comes from `surfaces/agent-dock.js::fit`: it previously
treated any `#tbar` height as a fixed bottom obstruction. The new front door
hosts that navigation in an in-flow footer disclosure. Its measured height
could move the launcher above the viewport. The repair only reserves space
for a visible fixed toolbar, keeps the legacy pre-toolbar fail-safe, and
bounds the launcher/dialog against the viewport and on-screen keyboard.
A tall expanded toolbar may be covered by the open dialog so its close
control and content remain reachable.

The second scrollbar came from a fixed 460px iframe inside a separately
scrolling, height-constrained `#adBody`. The frame now fills a shrinking flex
area, whose outer scrolling is disabled. Ordinary panels keep their own
scroll area; a help panel above an iframe can scroll independently if long.

New bee's dock uses the front door's light palette, readable controls and
44px targets. The same-origin embedded agent pages receive a scoped reading
style, updated on view changes. Their standalone pages, answers, identity
disclosures and capabilities are unchanged. Closing returns keyboard focus
to the launcher and updates its accessible expanded state. Escape also
works from inside the embedded page. The long-press click cannot reopen a
dock it has just hidden.
