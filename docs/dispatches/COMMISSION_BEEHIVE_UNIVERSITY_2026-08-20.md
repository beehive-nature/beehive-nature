## 2 · THE ONE PEDAGOGICAL LAW — every lesson ends in a verifiable act

Not a quiz. **A receipt the learner produces.**

- *Read a COA* → the learner opens our own COA and finds the panel undercount: the lab
  measured 10–16 cannabinoids and called it "total". They have now learned the
  `NotMeasured ≠ 0` law by catching it in the wild, on our material.
- *Read a nutrition label* → the learner computes their own body-scaled protein target
  and finds that most micronutrient RDAs do **not** scale — the distinction is the lesson.
- *Read evidence* → the learner re-runs a BiGen forest plot and watches the
  manufacturer-funded flag fire on our own commissioned COA.
- *Verify a name* → the learner folds a Merkle proof on `bTranslated` and watches a bad
  proof **refuse** rather than guess.
- *Publish a review* → the learner signs a review receipt on the Royal Review deck. That
  is graduation from bee level: they have contributed a verifiable artefact.

**A student who finishes a course has produced signed receipts, not a certificate.**
That is a credential nobody can print and nobody can fake — and it is the same
attestation grammar the Royal Guard already runs.

## 3 · THE FIRST FIVE COURSES, all already receipted

1. **How to read a lab report** — panels, LOQ, dry-weight vs as-received, mg/kg vs %,
   the acid/neutral 0.877 factor, and why the material tested (hull-on vs dehulled)
   changes everything. Source: this sprint's cannabinoid receipt, entire.
2. **How a requirement is set** — RDA vs AI vs EAR vs UL vs DV; why an AI means *the
   data were insufficient*; why "no UL" means absorptive saturation, not proven safety;
   which requirements scale with your body and which do not.
3. **How to be lied to with a true number** — the shortfall receipt is the whole
   syllabus: 96% below the EAR beside 5% at risk of deficiency, and why both are true.
4. **How to read a chain** — two independent oracles, a page must not be its own witness,
   failed fetches are failures. Source: the ERC-20i census.
5. **How colour communicates** — the pair law, the validated set, why a reordered palette
   loses its validation. Source: the design seat's critique of our own surface.
