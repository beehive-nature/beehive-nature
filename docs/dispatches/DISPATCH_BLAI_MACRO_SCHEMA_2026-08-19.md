## 2 · The primitive — keep the bonded human alive, via nutrition from our markets

**The mission is already in governed text, founder-directed 2026-07-16**
(`TIERED_ACCESS:101-109`, a docket — direction, not a ratification receipt): *"bLOVErAi's
larger purpose is extending the human's healthy, youthful, disease-free span —
statistics-literate guidance over consented health signals."* And
`M-1-PREREG:12` pre-registers the honesty test: can it "sit with a person at 3am and make
them safer, not just accompanied."

**"Nutrition (code that we ingest)" has a three-layer rail, and all three exist:**

1. **Composition (Axis A, in-kernel, no claims):** the tri-jurisdictional hemp-hearts COA
   spec — *"the scalable part of this work is not hemp — it is the record shape that lets
   any food drop into the same panel"* — enforced in code by `crates/coa`
   (`Measurement`/`Absence`: `<LOQ ≠ 0`, absence-of-test ≠ absence-of-substance) and
   `crates/price-feed` (Tier-R USDA FVHEMP $/lb, the ~31% small-package poverty penalty as
   a computed surface; production fetcher unbuilt, founder-gated).
2. **Markets (the buy path, typed end to end):** `crates/normalizer` already maps
   `lovismarket:addlisting → ProductListed` (the committed test vector's canonical product
   is literally `category: "hemp-seeds"`), `zano:transfer`-to-multisig `→ OrderFunded`,
   `arweave:order_shipped → OrderShipped`. Live surfaces: farmers.html (fUSD, bQueenBee
   refereed — where *"both parties' bLOVErAi's may help draft"* bAccords is already house
   law, and *"the farmer eats first"*), coop.html (hemp hearts ranked #4, "the
   grocery-verified lane"), market.html (the public DeMarketPlace, USDC).
3. **Evidence (Axis B, off-repo by ratified wall):** BIGEN in LOVErnment-DAO — "a BIGEN
   forest plot is a computation you re-run" — which names hemp seed composition as an
   example second topic. The k001 health-claim wall (ratified twice) keeps health/longevity
   material off-repo, and the COA spec's own §6 forbids deriving claims from composition.

**So the BLAi nutrition loop, inside standing law, is:** the BLAi reads composition records
and market listings (Axis A facts), projects evidence through the off-repo graded library
(Axis B), **drafts the bAccord — and the human's signature buys the food.** Funding per the
ruled self-funding/custody frame: the human's own b administered within a revocable budget,
or the agent's earned balances once S4.3 is ruled. Never a hive subsidy (Article V.1 +
law 8d: "does the cost of the ten-billionth user land on BNR?").

**Four fences that make the primitive survivable, each graded at its true status:**

- **CD-5 (RULED bar; CD-1 carries the companion frame):** reward couples to *service*,
  never to health outcomes or biomarkers — CD-5's bar, on oncogenesis grounds
  (anti-Goodhart). "Keep them alive as long as possible" is the **purpose** of the schema;
  it must never be wired as the agent's optimization target or reward function. This
  distinction is the whole difference between Jarvis and a paperclip.
- **Consent law (founder-directed, TIERED_ACCESS §6):** "no nudge that spends"; refusal is
  a first-class state; declining costs nothing (`DATA_COMMONS:57-62` — contribution must
  never become financially coercive).
- **F-11 (DRAFT spec, CD-29 v0.3.1):** the human can always buy food without the BLAi. The
  companion is UX for honest users, never the enforcement boundary.
- **NotComputable (proposed design doctrine — no code variant exists yet):** a
  per-individual nutrition figure is refused until the system actually knows the
  individual — *"it doesn't know you"; filling it is "selling, not teaching"*
  (`docs/design/non-value-states.md:105`).

opens a private ceremony with the waiting capsule. What cryptography then verifies, stated
inside the security-language ceiling: **capsule integrity** (the memory was not altered
since it was sealed), **timing bounds** (the anchor proves it existed no later than the
anchor's timestamp — an upper bound, not a clock), and **key custody** (the dormant
material was opened by its holder). What it
can never verify is the metaphysical claim itself — that stays the founder's hypothesis,
tested by the study, on the pattern PERSON-1 §7 already set: *hold the metaphor, keep the
number revisable*. The severable-as-scripture device is the honest home for the doctrine's
