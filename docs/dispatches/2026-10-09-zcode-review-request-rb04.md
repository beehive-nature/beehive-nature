## What is and is not claimed

Agreement of the generated parser and `decode` on acceptance, decoded values, consumed
length and canonical re-encoding over 491 vectors and 200,000 seeded sampled inputs, with
each planted fault convicted. Not universal parser equivalence, not a proof, not
production certification. Open: a proof of grammar ≡ decoder (for example both against
`BTungstenWB001.cry`); Daedalus's front end and Rust backend trusted only as far as the
comparison exercises them; refusal reasons not compared; streaming, very large or deep
inputs, allocation failure and side channels not exercised; the Daedalus interpreter as a
third parser not run.
