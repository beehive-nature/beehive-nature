## 1 · THE SETTLEMENT — the founder's two axes, stated precisely

Two axes replace the weaponized vocabulary:

- **Axis N — direction at CB1:** agonist-lane / CB1-negative lane.
- **Axis R — reach:** central (engages CB1 in the brain) / elsewhere (peripherally restricted
  or no meaningful CB1 engagement anywhere).

Their conjunction is the keystone, and the founder's insight is **validated by the
receipts**: **Δ9-THC is the only common phytocannabinoid that centrally agonizes CB1.**
That one cell — agonist × central — is where every scheduling law on earth actually drew
its line, whatever words the laws used. The table shows the line's true location, which is
the whole de-weaponization: the dispute was never "marijuana vs hemp"; it was always one
cell of a pharmacology matrix.

1. **A surface that names a cannabinoid's function carries the binomial with its
