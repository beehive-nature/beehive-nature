## The law that binds this order

- **Cite or the cell stays silent.** Every number carries its primary URL. A paywalled or
  unreachable source is a failed fetch, recorded as a failure — never a zero, never an
  estimate dressed as a measurement.
- **Never invent a body-size scaling.** Most micronutrient DRIs are group-level constants
  by age and sex; only some requirements genuinely scale with weight or height. Reporting
  a scaling the source does not state is precisely the fake science this build exists to
  refuse.
- **Axis A only.** Composition, published reference intakes, and regulatory values are
  facts we may carry. *Effect* — what a nutrient does to a body — stays off-repo and
  GRADE-graded (the k001 wall). The surface shows amounts, never outcomes.
