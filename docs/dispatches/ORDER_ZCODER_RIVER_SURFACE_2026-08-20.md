### 2 · The impactful graphic — RENDER THE SPACE, NOT THE LIST

### 4 · The contribution path — autonomous on a static site

existing rails. **The page never posts anything itself and never holds a key.** A
withdrawal is a marker with a date: the surface stops rendering, the history stays.
