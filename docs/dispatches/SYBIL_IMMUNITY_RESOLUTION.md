# SYBIL IMMUNITY RESOLUTION

Seat: Goose Seat 1. Date: 2026-08-10.
Founder question: how to prove 1-in-10B uniqueness without BNRoSe resources?

## THE ANSWER (already designed)

The Founder asked how a guest proves uniqueness without resources. The answer exists across two existing specs and does NOT require new work for alpha.

## 1. bRespect Sybil Ruling (docs/brespect-sybil-unlock.md)

A multiple bDiD is UNDECIDABLE. No issuer, no registry, self-certifying hash. The system does not detect duplicates.
Instead: an unvouched root sits at rung 0 forever. Lifetime ceiling 42 b, 10 percent annual cap, no self-borrow.
Ten thousand sybils are free, instantaneous, undetectable, and worth 420,000 b of proven resource contribution. That is a labor-market problem, not an identity problem.
The vouch graph (hybrid stake-and-vouch) makes only one root economically alive per human.

## 2. PoUL Proof of Unique Life (dockets/PROOF_OF_UNIQUE_LIFE_design_note.md)

Personhood is NOT a gate passed once. It is a posterior that deepens over a lifetime.
P(unique living human given all attestations) is a confidence that narrows with independent signals.
Signal families (opt-in, consented): hardware continuity, temporal persistence, liveness/biometric, behavioral continuity, social attestation, contribution over time.
TIME is the un-buyable signal: a sybil can buy N devices in an afternoon, not a decade of one key consistently sensing and contributing.
Low confidence means less-attested, NOT less-human. PoUL gates privileged actions (governance, unbounded spend), never participation, voice, or dignity.

## HOW THIS MAPS TO STAGING

Alpha guest: creates bDiD locally (passkey). Zero resources. Sits at rung 0. Browse, read, observe. No value at risk. Sybil-irrelevant.
Alpha paid: user acquires A (self-funded). Participates in compute via Buzz. Contribution over time earns signals. Vouches from existing unique users deepen confidence.
Beta (b): unlocks based on confidence threshold, not a gate. Rung-gated ceiling prevents sybil farming. PoUL confidence plus Respect equals unlock weight.

## RESOLUTION

How to prove 1-in-10B uniqueness without resources:
1. You do NOT prove it upfront. A guest is accepted at rung 0 with zero proof.
2. Sybils at rung 0 are free but inert. 42 b lifetime, 10 percent annual cap. Not worth farming.
3. Uniqueness confidence deepens over time through weighted attestations (PoUL).
4. The vouch graph provides the first uniqueness signal with NO resources needed from the new user.
5. b unlocks at a confidence threshold, not a gate. Time plus contribution plus vouches equals confidence.

This is already designed. It needs no new spec for alpha. Implementation is a beta milestone.
