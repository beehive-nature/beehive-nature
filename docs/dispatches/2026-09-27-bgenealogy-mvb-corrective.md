## 2. "DIED WITHOUT ISSUE" — scope doctrine, applied (Walter de Mandeville)

The spine's g25←g26 (Walter's parent = Christine FitzRobert "Countess of Essex") rides on FamilySearch/WikiTree making Walter (b.c.1220, Staffordshire per FS) a son of **William de Mandeville** by **Christine/Christina fitzRobert (= fitzWalter, of the Magna Carta baron's family)**. The last earl of that line, **William de Mandeville (d. 8 Jan 1227)**, is traditionally recorded **died without issue**. Per the founder's doctrine, that phrase is a **scoped feudal assertion, not a biological census**:

- A d.s.p. in CP/Inq context typically certifies **no legitimate surviving heirs claiming the honour** (the earldom escheated/went collateral in 1227). It does **not** exclude: children who died young, children born outside marriage, or children recorded nowhere in the feudal line.
- Conversely, it **contradicts a legitimate-son Walter who survived to 1267 and left issue** — a legitimate son of the last earl would have been the heir. No affirmative record of such an heir exists.
