## What landed

### S2 — jams cross-origin query overrides closed (security)

`surfaces/jams.html`: `?manifest=` and `?store=` are now **same-origin
affordances only** — a `sameOriginOverride()` guard resolves each param and
ignores it fail-closed when its origin differs, with a visible
`REFUSED · cross-origin manifest/store override ignored` row in the room log
(no silent masking). The same-origin path is deliberately preserved: the
store-reader battery (`zcode-jams-store-reader-check.mjs`) and local fixture
workflows ride it, and it is not attack surface (an attacker cannot plant
bytes on the estate origin).

Closed vectors, probe-proven: the crafted-link content spoof (attacker fields
under SKAISTS branding), the both-ends "verified" chip lighting, and the
verify-tap beacon GET to an attacker origin (zero requests left the page in
the hardened probe run). The integrity gate itself is unchanged and still
refuses mismatched sha256 through the same-origin path.

### S3 — rails badge escapes the soul before innerHTML (security, shared shell)

`surfaces/rails-badge.js`: the stored `bnr_soul` value is entity-escaped
before interpolation into the badge markup (both the `<b>` label and the
fingerprint tooltip). The TOFU fingerprint hash still computes on the RAW
value — identifier stability unchanged; the escaped form is display-only.
Probe-proven: stored markup now renders as literal text, no execution.

### P1 — referrer suppression cluster-wide (privacy, shared shell)

`surfaces/tour.js`: the external-link delegation law now adds BOTH
`noopener` and `noreferrer` (token-deduped — no duplicate rel tokens on
re-clicks). `plur.html`, `jams.html`, `blanguage.html` gain
`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">`, matching watch.html — the
cluster standard is now uniform: no page URL travels to any third party as a
Referer, including blanguage's ~50 research links.

### T1 — plur's d.plur.* sections wired (the substantive finding)

`surfaces/plur.html`: 15 `data-key` attributes renamed to `data-i18n`
(the corpus already carried every key ×28 — the door-intro fold had happened;
only the wiring was missing). Three structural notes:

- the talk `<h2>` head text is wrapped in a keyed `<span>` (leaf-first): the
  old shape put keys on both the h2 and its child span, and a parent
  textContent swap would wipe the keyed child;
- `d.plur.stone.dock` is left **unwired by design**: its paragraph carries two
  live links, and a plain-text corpus cell swap would destroy them — the
  honest trade is an English line (counted unkeyed by the coverage counter)
  instead of dead links in 28 tongues; the corpus key stays for a future
  fragment-aware render;
- the three bare section-marker attributes (`d.plur.talk` etc.) are dropped —
  they never had corpus keys and `estate-source` requires every `data-i18n`
  key to exist.

**Where the fix lives, and why not the shared shell:** teaching lang.js to
consume `data-key` would change apply/census semantics for every surface —
and fieldnotes.html carries 4 nested keyed holders today, so a leaf-safety
rule in the shared shell has fleet-wide blast radius. The attribute rename
achieves the identical reader outcome with zero shared-shell risk. lang.js is
untouched.

Known cosmetic: translated renders of `d.plur.roses.h`, `d.plur.roses.why`,
`d.plur.talk.sub` flatten their `<b>` emphasis (corpus cells are plain text;
the markup-aware swap restores English exactly, including markup). Cells
carrying markup are lawful per the corpus trust basis — a future sweep, not a
blocker.

### N1 — the dead rose links (rider)

`surfaces/plur.html`: `qrroses.html` → `blight/qrroses.html`,
`qrtree.html` → `blight/qrtree.html`. Both were 404 live on skaists.dev
(curl-proven in the review dispatch); all plur internal links now resolve.

### U1 — left open, deliberately

The Open-chat-on-hosts-without-`/join/` gap is neither a security finding nor
a shared-shell fix, and its cure is a product call (disable the button on
unavailable health vs. an honest in-frame message) that belongs to the watch
surface's owner lane. Recorded here so it does not vanish.
