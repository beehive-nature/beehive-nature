graphic artist who finds Apple clear and familiar. She is the living matriarch;
frontend usability is the founder's highest product priority. New bee is the
approved default: dignified plain language, readable text, generous controls,

**Your implementation mission:** build coherent user-to-user, user-to-agent,
and agent-to-agent social journeys across the organism. Begin with a small
vertical slice a person can actually use: discover a community or participant,
understand who/what they are addressing, converse where transport exists,
understand delivery state, and return home. Make the human/agent distinction
explicit. Show local draft, queued, sent, delivered and failed only when the
corresponding mechanism supplies evidence. Keep consent and agent delegation
scoped and visible. A local composer, an iframe prompt bridge, a mocked reply
and a live network conversation must describe their actual capabilities.

Map every proposed mechanism to the seven constitutional primitives:
**Identity, Intent, Event, Evidence, Knowledge, Resource, Settlement.**
Capability is reserved, not an eighth primitive to invent. Self-learning does
not bypass amendment authority. The 1,000-year/10-billion-user objective is
a design target to test, not a result to put in marketing copy.

Use fresh, bounded worker sessions and explicit file ownership. Reserve

use smaller focused sessions for isolated copy or styling work. Report actual
model/effort choices rather than inventing another seat's settings. Integrate
small complete slices; do not create a fleet of overlapping rewrites.

Follow the repository's build, registry, secret and identity gates. Changes

`loVis waTer <loviswater44@gmail.com>`; identify your machine seat consistently

Production is read-first; a mutation needs its concrete change receipt and

existing leashed transport to the bootstrap box. Existing dry-run services
remain intentional. Read the specific operational receipt before any ops work.

Your first return should be a concise source-cited map, the lane you are
claiming, your fleet allocation, and a reviewable first slice. Every completed

within the founder's authorization; ask only for information or authority that
