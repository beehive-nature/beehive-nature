## 2 · Phase 2 — BEELOG (`ac43270a0`)

**Files**: `crates/bzdid/src/beelog.rs`, `tests/beelog_independent.rs`.

RFC 6962 tree over the identity's STORED op bytes (leaf index IS op
seq; genesis at leaf 0): `0x00`/`0x01` domain prefixes, MTH split at
the largest power of two, empty root = SHA-256(""). Head = {size,
root}. **The RFC texts were fetched from the RFC editor mid-lane and
implemented line-by-line** — after two from-memory derivations of the
consistency verifier were proven wrong by the tests and discarded.
The load-bearing details, named so nobody "simplifies" them later: the
consistency proof is SUBPROOF WITH the is-original flag (the bottom-out
node; without it, spanning sizes like 3→4 are unverifiable), and both
verifiers are the RFC 9162 §2.1.3.2/§2.1.4.2 walks (fn/sn inclusion;
fr/sr two-hash consistency with the power-of-two prepend).

What the tree buys over the ops' own prev-chain: O(log n) inclusion
evidence and SIZE-BASED append-only evidence — `head_relation`
classifies two heads as Equal / Extends / Fork / Shorter / Unproven; a
longer head with no valid proof is Unproven, NEVER silently accepted.
Recovery: a wallet holding head(k) catches up to head(n) by arithmetic
alone, no leaf re-read, then verifies ops against the adopted head.

**Independent verifier**: bottom-up pair-and-carry levels vs the
library's top-down recursion — agree on EVERY root and path for sizes
0..65, across 120-checkpoint random growth (every pair reconciles),
under 300 deterministic mutations (verify ⇒ byte-identical original).
