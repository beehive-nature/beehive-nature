## Choreography for Grok

Treat the gesture as one continuous movement by the same two partners.
Maintain each person's hand, skin tone, wrist orientation and bracelet
identity through the sequence. Do not substitute unrelated posed gestures
or a sequence of generic hand icons.

1. **P — Peace:** the two partners' V-sign fingertips meet.
2. **L — Love:** each partner shapes half the heart. Their index fingertips
   and thumb tips touch, creating one heart together. The contact matters.
3. **U — Unity:** the fingers move into each other's gaps and interlock in
   a clasp. The founder explicitly selected interlocking/clasping; flat
   prayer hands are not the requested Unity demonstration.
4. **R — Respect:** hold that joined clasp while the kandi passes over the
   hands to the receiving wrist. The final panel shows the received
   pink/mint bracelet around the receiver's wrist beside their original
   blue/lavender bracelet; the giver's wrist is now bare. No bracelet rests
   on the knuckles. Keep the same clasp rather than resetting to another handshake.

For movement work, interpolate the actual hands/fingers between these
positions. A fade between static cards is not proof of continuous hand
motion. Keep letter and word legible alongside each movement. Offer a
reader-controlled replay/pause and a static sequence for reduced motion;
the gesture must remain understandable without animation.

Suggested accessible captions: "Touch peace signs", "Make one heart
together", "Interlock your fingers", "Pass the kandi". When integrated,
keep explanatory copy in translatable HTML as well as the English concept
art. Preserve New bee's readability and the same meaning in every skin.

## Sources and review limits

The founder's demonstration correction is the design authority for this
asset. Community descriptions vary in whether the clasp is named under
Unity or Respect. The [iHeartRaves trading guide](https://iheartraves.com/en-au/blogs/post/how-to-trade-kandi-at-a-rave)
describes joining/interlocking at Unity and transferring the bracelet at
Respect; [USC's first-person folklore records](https://folklore.usc.edu/tag/edm-culture/)
also record a palms-together then interlocking variant. These were consulted
to understand the gesture, not to override the founder's chosen sequence.

Four generated states were inspected directly: the initial illustration,
a palms-together alternative, the founder-corrected clasp, and the final
wrist-to-wrist transfer correction. Only
the selected final image is added to the repository. The PNG is a visual
demonstration, not motion capture, anatomical certification or a deployed
interaction. No browser QA, service operation, public campaign or message
to a marketing audience was performed.
