## ROUND 3 (stateful rules landed, 2026-09-05 ~01:30Z) — OCI drops inbound IP-fragment tails

48-byte ACK Initials now reach the daemon, and tcpdump at the NIC shows the
server's **4096-byte ServerHello flights arriving as first-fragments**. The

non-initial fragments; the box's own iptables REJECT chain (correctly, per
the founder's note) stays the real door-keeper. Scoped alternative:
