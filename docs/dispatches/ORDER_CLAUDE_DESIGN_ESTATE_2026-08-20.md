## 0 · What "matches its backend" means, concretely

The backend's caliber is *earned honesty*: every number traced, every absence rendered,
every state marked three ways, corrections in public. A gold-standard front end does not
decorate that — **it makes the honesty legible at first glance.** The bar: a stranger who
has never heard of this project lands on the hub and, within one screen, understands both
*what this is* and *that it can be checked*. Today's surfaces pass every truth test and
fail that first-glance test; that gap is the assignment.

## 1 · The asks, in order

1. **D1 — the macro token sheet, brought to gold standard.** The sprint's D1 exists as a
   brief; deliver it as the worked artboard: surfaces (light+dark), ink scale, the accent
   family, **validated dataviz steps** (the six-checks law, receipts), type scale on the
   IBM Plex Mono lineage, the 720px→1000px column discipline, card + nav grammar, and
   **non-value-state renderings as first-class design objects** (absent · not measured ·
   not computable · single-source · floor) — the states our surfaces actually live in and
   most designs never draw.
2. **The hub as the flagship.** One artboard redesigning `surfaces/index.html` — 38 cards,
   7 wings — so the estate reads as *one instrument*, not a link list. The founder browses
   on an A16: mobile-first is a constraint, not a variant.
3. **One worked surface at gold standard** (recommend bFood — it is the pyramid's successor
   and the most information-dense page we have; its 31-cell comb, ledger, fat table, quest
   ladder and dials are the hardest design problem in the estate and solve everywhere else
   if solved there).
4. **D5 register templates** — already ordered; unchanged; may fold into this pass.
5. **The motion/interaction layer, minimal and honest** — loading states that never lie
   (no skeleton-as-truth), live-fetch failure states (the museum observation in the estate
   review), and transitions that respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 2 · Laws that bind (from the seed brief + house rulings, unchanged)

Colors never operate singular (pair law; validated sets only; identity never rides color
alone) · **the LIGHT wing's identity is founder-emotional and ruled — restyle around it,
never redesign it away** · casings are the payload, transcribed exactly · one axis per
chart · WCAG AA floor on every composited pair, both themes · the corpus law: every
colour-communicating state's label is a corpus record in the reader's three language roles
(father · mother · students) — include one tri-role specimen · **flag collisions in the
artboard notes and stop; the founder rules.**

## 3 · Not in scope

No implementation (artboards + specs; the implementation seats execute) · no new hues
outside the macro ramps · no redesign of any surface's *content semantics* — the numbers,
brakes, and absence laws are settled and are the point · nothing that resolves an open
gate silently (U-1, BF-1, PI-1…).
