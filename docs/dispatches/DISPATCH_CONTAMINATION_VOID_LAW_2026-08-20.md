## 0 · THE LAW

**A study whose material is proven adulterated is not weak evidence — it is void
evidence**, and it is voided **in public, with the proof**, while the high-confidence
corpus stands visibly beside it. The integrity layer already distinguishes *unclear /
not_assessed* from *retracted* (defaults unfavourable, retraction sweeps weekly). This law
adds a third, stronger state:

| verdict | trigger | mechanics |
|---|---|---|
| `VOIDED_CONTAMINATED` | an **adulterant assay receipt** proves the studied material carried contaminants — petrochemical solvent residues, synthetic cannabinoids passed as plant, pesticide loads, or any adulterant class the assay names | the study card carries the verdict with the **assay receipt inline** (analyte, method, LOQ, lab, date — the COA discipline applied to the literature); the tier table drops its claims from any aggregate rendering; **the void never deletes the study** — it strikes it, visibly, with the proof attached |

**Voided-not-deleted is the point.** A silent removal is indistinguishable from
suppression and invites the exact conspiracy reading this project exists to starve. The
public strike with receipts — the museum's show-the-errors doctrine, applied to other
people's labs — is both stronger *and* fairer: any party can contest the assay, and the
contest is a new receipt, not an edit war.

## 1 · WHY THIS LANE IS LOAD-BEARING FOR THE FOUNDER'S THESIS

The founder's economic case (the RELAY_SEED arbitrage) and the ACiD protocol both lean on
one implied claim: **that a substantial fraction of the cannabis literature's negative or
null findings studied contaminated, degraded, or mislabelled material** — extracts cut
with diluents, products with solvent residues, "cannabis" studies that studied synthetics.
If that is true even partly, the contamination-void lane is not an attack on the
literature — **it is the rescue of it**: separating "the molecule failed" from "the
material was not the molecule." The honesty requirements that bind us apply to the voids
too:

1. **A void requires an assay, not a vibe.** Suspicion, funding flags, and methodological
   dislike do not void — only an adulterant receipt does. The manufacturer-funded flag
   keeps firing symmetrically **including on our own commissioned material** (SCHEMA Law
   A — the negative control).
2. **Grading survives the void.** `VOIDED_CONTAMINATED` is about the *material*; it never
   converts into an automatic claim about the *question* — the molecule's status moves
   only through the graded corpus that remains.
3. **Our own studies are voidable by the same law.** The first application candidate,
   when our commissioned COA lands, is our own lane: any lot that fails the adulterant
   screen is published as failed. A void law that only points outward is marketing.

## 2 · WHAT LANDS WHERE

- **BiGen integrity schema**: gains `material_integrity: { adulterant_assay: receipt|null,
  verdict: clean|voided_contaminated|unassessed }` — unassessed by default, exactly like
  every other integrity field.
- **The tier tables / evidence maps**: voided entries render struck-through with the assay
  link, never silently omitted; the source table carries the verdict column.
- **The high-confidence corpus beside it**: the founder's "alongside our BiGen high
  confidence research/labs/data" — the void page and the confidence ledger are one view:
  *here is what was struck and why; here is what stands and why.* Two columns, same
  honesty, no middle shelf.
- **Assay receipts ride the COA discipline**: analyte, method, LOQ, uncertainty, lab
  accreditation — the tri-jurisdictional spec's law, applied to every void.
