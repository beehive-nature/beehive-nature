**Founder directive, verbatim (2026-08-20):** *"looks great and we will use it for any
similar offers from other adapter service providers."* The Base Batches kit
(`BASE_BATCHES_SUBMISSION_KIT.md` + `surfaces/b4b.html`) is now the reusable pattern.
This file is the generalized anatomy; a provider-specific kit is a copy + §3 filled.

## §1 · THE LAWS THAT MAKE IT WORK (never cut these)

1. **The evidence link is the product.** One live, public page (Pages-served, house
   grammar, tokens.css) holding our *measured receipts of that provider's own
   chain/protocol* — taken before we applied, two oracles where chain facts are
   involved, every claim linked. The page closes with the dare: *verify — that is the
   whole application.*
2. **Eligibility mapped honestly, gap included.** Where we don't meet a pillar (their
   chain-first, their stage), the application answers with a **commitment plan under a
   founder gate** — never hand-waving, never chain migration (the art law: ERC-20i art
   never crosses a bridge; applications move read layers and products, never art).
3. **Every milestone a receipt.** The plan's weeks each end in a landable, verifiable
   artifact. "We would rather be proven wrong in week 3 than flattered in week 8."
4. **Founder-gates the irreversible parts:** equity/counsel (an investment ≠ a grant),
   entity name, travel, any signature. The kit never signs.
5. **After-submit, the application becomes a receipt** (confirmation pasted into the
   kit's §-equivalent; the wall is truth).
6. **Synergy, not flattery:** only lanes we were already building, on rails that
   already read that provider — "the program accelerates work with receipts behind it."

## §2 · THE KIT SKELETON (per provider)

1. Send checklist (order of operations; note draft-saving behavior of their form).
2. Written submission paste-blocks: one-liner · what we're building · why now · why
   *them* (their named bets × our receipts on their rails) · traction · team (founder +
   the organism, two-way verification culture) · milestones · the ask beyond capital
   (intros + pressure-testing).
3. Links block: evidence page · estate · repo · receipts dir · the provider-specific
   receipts.
4. Video script (~100 s): open on the receipts frame; middle on their chain measured;
   close on the verify dare.
5. Founder-only fields (entity / counsel / travel / contact).
6. After-submit receipt step.

## §4 · WHEN AN OFFER ARRIVES (the 4-hour rule)

1. Verify the offer is real (two-source; scams arrive as "accelerators" too).
2. Copy this template + the provider row of §3 → draft kit + evidence page.
3. Gates to founder (equity? entity? exclusivity clauses? — **any exclusivity demand
   hits the portfolio law: rails are a portfolio, never a single bet; flag, never sign**).
4. Land, push, walk the smoke — then the founder sends.
