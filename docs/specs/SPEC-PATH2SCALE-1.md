## 1 · THE SAFETY WALLS BEFORE THE MACHINES — a lane that feeds animals carries animal laws

1. **THC never enters the canine lane.** Canine marijuana toxicosis is a receipted
   clinical reality that grows as cannabis products spread ("veterinarians are seeing more
   cases of toxicosis" — *Animals* 2021, PMID 33804793; clinical series: PMIDs 39744711,
   34786688). Under the functional-naming settlement this is one cell: the **central CB1
   agonist** cell — the same cell every human scheduling law drew — is simply **walled off
   for dogs by construction**, not by dosage caution. The canine cannabinoid lane, if it
   ever carries actives, is the CB1-negative/peripheral class only, and even that is
   veterinary Axis B: graded, off-surface, **no dose ever renders on a surface**.
2. **Cats are not small dogs** (PS-2): feline metabolism receipts before anything.
3. **The cannabinoid demand dial is a DIAL, not a number.** No authority sets a
   cannabinoid requirement for any species (the routing ruling's §2.2, restated); a number
   exists only if CB-1's pre-registered protocol produces one. Until then the demand model
   parameterizes it and says so.
