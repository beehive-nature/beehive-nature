## The one measurement nobody else publishes

Every public cannabinoid figure describes inflorescence, plant, resin, or extract —
**none describes the material we actually eat** (hemp heart / seed), and no commercial
COA panel exceeds 15–16 analytes against ~160 identified (the panel-undercount receipt,
`37a432f`, exhibit 002). One commissioned assay closes both gaps at once, on our own
lot, with the result published as a register record anyone can cite.

## The ask, in priority order

1. **Untargeted LC-HRMS** on the lot (the Monti 2022 pattern: targeted + untargeted on
   the same sample; report every annotated compound with confidence level). This is the
   headline number: **"total (n of ~160 known)" rendered with our own n.**
2. **Maximal targeted panel** as the calibration floor (≥ the AOAC 12; state the panel
   size on every line — the display law).
3. **Matrix = hulled hemp heart AND the lot's oil fraction**, separately reported — the
   seed ceiling question (contamination mechanism vs panel width) is answerable only on
   seed matrix, and no source in the literature provides it.
4. **LC-CAD unified-calibration total** if the lab offers it — the most promising untried
   route to a true mass total (Jornet-Martinez 2023: no reference method exists; Fast
   Blue B is a "rough estimation" and is NOT an acceptable substitute).
