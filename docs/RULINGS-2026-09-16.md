## R1. IDENTITY DOMAINS — THREE, NEVER CONFUSED (DC-2 — RATIFIED, text preserved verbatim)

1. Human sovereign identity is a Layer-0 bzDiD keypair (SPEC-VAULTA-IDENTITY-1 §0.1).
   No public chain account is required for it, and no permanent mapping between a human
   identity and any wallet or stored object may appear in public settlement data or
   public receipts.
2. Agent/service identity MAY hold public chain accounts (WALLET-LEDGER roster under the
   2026-08-29 autonomy ruling) with bounded authority and auditable settlement identity.
   Nothing may make such a public mapping a REQUIRED payment mechanism: payments flow
   through purpose-scoped capabilities (capAssert, linkauth, capability tiers), never
   through a mandatory universal public payer identity.
3. Transaction/receipt privacy is governed independently (SPEC-SPEND-RECEIPT-1 §3a):
   private by default; visibility is access-control, not a label; widening is consented
   and non-retroactive.

No clause weakens another; in particular (1) is never relaxed to resolve a conflict with (2).
Founder ratification: "Human sovereign identity, agent/service identity, and transaction/
receipt privacy are distinct domains. Public agent identities never weaken human privacy."
