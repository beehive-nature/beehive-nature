# THE RAVER REGISTER LAW — 🎛, ruled by its native speaker

**Standing law, 2026-08-21.** The estate's three-register toggle (🐝 bee · 🎛 raver ·
⚗ cypherpunk, `surfaces/register.js`) treats the raver register as a **tongue with a
living native speaker: the founder, thirty years on the floor.** His own words are the
register's constitution, verbatim:

> *"kind of like an antiracist, voluntarist, hippy autistic PLUR vibe"*

## What each word binds, in drafting law

- **PLUR** — Peace · Love · Unity · Respect. The raver register never gatekeeps,
  never sneers, never talks down. Everyone on the floor is already in. The door
  price here is zero and the prose says so warmly.
- **antiracist** — every tongue equal is not a feature, it is the floor. The
  26-tongue corpus, "all that serves none that doesn't," is this word in code.
  Raver prose never assumes whose English it is talking to.
- **voluntarist** — consent-first, always. Nothing is done *to* the reader:
  every load is on gesture, every ceremony is opt-in, withdrawal is a first-class
  state. Raver prose invites; it never herds.
- **hippy** — the commons is real: open source, keyless, free to look, built for
  the poor starving artist. The register is allowed joy, warmth, and lowercase.
- **autistic** — and here the beautiful convergence: the estate's honesty laws
  ARE autistic-friendly design. **Literal language. Explicit mechanisms. No
  idioms that lie, no signal prettier than the truth, no hidden meanings.**
  Sensory-honest by measurement: `prefers-reduced-motion` honored everywhere,
  nothing on the estate flashes faster than 3 Hz (the festival rig's fastest
  full cycle is 5.2 s), motion pauses when the tab hides.

## The attestation lane (how the native speaker helps, one line at a time)

Same law as every tongue: machine drafts wear ⚙, the native speaker's word
upgrades them. The lane is deliberately tiny:

1. Flip any page to 🎛.
2. When a line reads like a tourist wrote it, say the real line — any channel,
   any length, even three words.
3. The seat folds it in **verbatim** (casings are the payload, never normalized)
   and it wears his ✓.

A vocabulary seed accelerates everything: ten real phrases from the floor —
what you'd actually shout to say *this is safe*, *this is free*, *everyone's
in*, *watch the rig*, *mind each other* — and the seats retrofit every raver
block estate-wide in that vocabulary instead of guessing at it.

## Binding on

Every `data-reg="raver"` block estate-wide, every future raver draft by any
seat, and the register.js law header at its next functional change (the pointer
lands there without a cache-cascade until then). One set of constants stands:
registers restyle prose and density, **never numbers** — PLUR changes the voice,
not the facts.
