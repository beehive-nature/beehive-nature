# REGISTER — the serving basis and the presentation law (founder-hardwired 2026-08-20)

## 1 · The serving scalar — HARDWIRED

Founder (King Bee), verbatim: *"100 grams of hemp hearts per 80 kg adult hardwired king
bee but present to the world/users the nutritional facks not more triggering hemp and
cannabis everywhere"*

**The serving basis: 100 g per 80 kg adult = 1.25 g per kg of body weight per day.**

This answers the founder's earlier note that *"that is a serving"* — the reference
serving now scales with the person, which is what the height/weight request asked for and
what the previous surface lacked.

**Its status, stated so the surface never blurs two different kinds of number:**

| number | what it is | who set it |
|---|---|---|
| **1.25 g/kg/day hemp hearts** | a **house serving basis** — the project's own reference portion | **founder-hardwired**, not a published standard, always labeled as the house basis |
| RDA / AI / UL / DV | published reference intakes | NASEM/IOM, FDA — cited per value |
| per-100 g composition | measured food content | USDA FDC — cited per value |

Rendering the house serving as though it were a published requirement would be exactly
the category error this project refuses. Labeled honestly, it is simply what it is: the
portion the house recommends, scaled to the body — and the reader can see every published
number beside it and judge for themselves.

**Worked:** a 60 kg adult → 75 g/day · 80 kg → 100 g/day · 100 kg → 125 g/day.
The animal arms (dog/cat/cow, per the environmental commission) scale on the same
principle by body weight, with species requirements from AAFCO/NRC rather than human DRIs.

## 2 · THE PRESENTATION LAW — lead with nutrition, never with cannabis

Founder, same ruling: *"present to the world/users the nutritional facks not more
triggering hemp and cannabis everywhere"*

**This is now binding on every user-facing surface.** The food is presented as **food** —
a complete-nutrition seed sold in ordinary grocery stores — with the nutritional facts
forward. Cannabis framing is not foregrounded, not decorative, and never the hook.

**Why this is also the strongest position, not merely the safest:** the moment a page
leads with cannabis, a large fraction of readers stop evaluating the nutrition and start
evaluating their prior about cannabis. The argument never gets heard. Leading with the
itemized RDI arithmetic and the shortfall data means the reader is judging **numbers they
can check**, which is the only ground this project ever wanted to fight on.

**And it converges with the ratified k001 wall by a different road:** the wall already
requires that composition facts carry the surface while effect claims live off-repo and
graded. The presentation law adds that even the *composition* facts most likely to
trigger a category response stay out of the headline. Same discipline, now stated as
product doctrine as well as legal doctrine.

**Practical consequences for the rebuild:**
- Headline: complete nutrition, exact per-body requirements, the shortfall arbitrage.
- Cannabinoid composition remains available and cited (it is an honest fact about the
  food, and the GRAS 765 material verifies why it is trace-level in dehulled hearts) —
  **available, not foregrounded**; a reader who goes looking finds it, a reader who came
  for nutrition is never handed it.
- The dose/route protocol stays routed to BIGEN pre-registration (RULING_CANNABINOID_
  PROTOCOL_ROUTING_2026-08-20), untouched by this ruling.
- Naming: "hemp hearts" / "hulled hemp seed" — the grocery names, which is what the food
  is actually sold as.
