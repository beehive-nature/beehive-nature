# RULINGS-2026-10-04 — post-quantum polish: readiness, the phrase, the QR words

**Provenance.** After the post-quantum polish pass (main `80986e806`), Seat 3 escalated three
questions and recommended an answer to each. Founder order, 2026-10-04, verbatim:

> "go ahead full steam on all you recommendations in the my rulings:"

…naming the three questions below. These rulings are FOUNDER-RATIFIED by that order. Each one is
the recommendation as Seat 3 wrote it, recorded here so the code has a ruling to cite.

## R1 — `pq.ready` means a PQ successor is named

**Question.** SPEC-VAULTA-IDENTITY-1 §2 and §6 gave `pq.ready` the meaning "this record CAN be
superseded by a PQ key" and asked for `pq.ready=true` from record one. Every classical key can be
superseded later, so that claim told a reader nothing and no code could check it. The relay's v2
envelope (`crates/wallet-relay/src/envelope.rs`) already said less: true only when the key is
ML-DSA-65 or a well-formed `bzpq1` successor is named.

**Ruling.** The spec takes the code's narrow meaning (SPEC-VAULTA-IDENTITY-1 v0.3 §2.1, §6.3).
`pq.ready` means "a PQ successor is named"; it is not a claim that the envelope is covered by a PQ
signature, and the envelope does not bind the successor to the key holder (that is the holder's own
SPEC-BPQ-1 §3 binding). v1 records are never rewritten; their `pq.ready:true` is read as unchecked,
and the relay echoes a v1 enrollment without its `pq` block.

## R2 — "only me" opens from the phrase alone

**Question.** SPEC-BPQ-1 §1 said readers need only the object and their own phrase, but every PQ
key, the "only me" vault key included, was derived with the context `pq:<soul name>`, and the soul
name lived only in the browser. A browser that forgot the name could not open the owner's own files.

**Ruling.** A reserved context `root` (outside the `pq:` namespace, so it never equals a persona)
derives a phrase-only vault key under the same frozen label. The wallet seals every "only me" slot
with it, so those files open from the phrase alone. Files shared to a persona through an X-Wing
slot still need that persona's soul name (the name on its card); the wallet asks for it when no
known name opens a file. Objects sealed before this ruling open with the persona context, and the
wallet keeps trying every known persona. No object-format change; SPEC-BPQ-1 §1 and §2 say exactly
this.

## R3 — the QR check is six words, not four digits

**Question.** The QR bridge's pre-check was four digits from the desktop's ephemeral key and the
session id. Someone able to swap the QR under a genuine desktop can read those digits and grind a
key that matches them in about ten thousand tries.

**Ruling.** The check becomes six words from the BIP-39 English list (66 bits), derived from a
domain-separated SHA-256 over every QR field that matters (the desktop's ephemeral, the session id
and the X-Wing commitment). Grinding a match is out of reach for the life of a session. The words
catch a QR swapped under a genuine desktop; they cannot catch a page that is itself fake and shows
its own QR and words, and the code says so. No QR format change.
