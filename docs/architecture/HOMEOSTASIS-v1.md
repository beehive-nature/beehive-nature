## The thesis
The ecosystem is not a collection of repos — it is one organism whose organs
already exist. HOMEOSTASIS is the name for the state we maintain: every organ
senses, decides, attests, creates, trades, and remembers through two shared
protocols, and no organ's failure can kill the organism.

## The two connective protocols (the interop law)
1. **Addressable artifacts.** Every meaningful object in any organ carries a
   deep link (`?seed=` `?score=` `?piece=` `?order=`) — humans share rooms,
   agents crawl the corpus. One grammar across all eight repos.
2. **Escrow events.** Every commitment (offer, accord, offtake, vote-backed
   spend) is an event in the engine schema (buyer_did, amount, asset, guards
   with visible math, carrier, tracking). Refusals are amber, never red.

## The constitution (standing, cross-organ)
- Anchor-truth: the chain is the truth; indexes and UIs are caches.
- Locked-only, exact amounts, cushions shown, art never touches pools.
- Static-first until a contract signs; no backend before its event exists.
- done = deployed + verified + named as exactly what it is.
- Every organ keeps its own frequency; the tour bar (or its local twin) links
  them; no organ absorbs another — inclusivity by architecture, not by merger.
