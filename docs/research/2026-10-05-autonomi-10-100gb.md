# Autonomi at 10 GB and 100 GB: limits, statistics, tungsten test

Research note, 2026-10-05. Quote only: for this note nothing was paid, stored, signed or uploaded, and no key or wallet was touched.
MB = 10^6 B, GB = 10^9 B, GiB = 2^30 B. Upstream code is pinned to ant-client `db85c72`, the exact source of the browser kit we vendor ([VENDOR.md:8][o-vendor]). That commit is 13 commits after the `ant-cli-v0.3.9` tag (`git describe`: `ant-cli-v0.3.9-13-gdb85c72`); its crate version still reads 0.3.9, but it is not the v0.3.9 release.
Prices are the 2026-10-05 reads: ANT $0.03188575, ETH $2,719.79, Arbitrum One gas 0.02004 gwei ([receipt][rcpt] `usd`, `gas`).

## In plain words

- **Yes, 1 GB is a coded limit, but only in Autonomi's browser kit**, which is the code our wallet page runs. View source: [limits.js line 2][k-limits]. The live page serves this file byte for byte ([receipt][rcpt] `provenance_liveEqualsRepo`). Autonomi's own core sets the same number and gives the reason at [browser.rs lines 34-38][u-browser]: in a browser, uploads can stream through browser storage, but a complete download still has to fit in memory.
- **The native client codes no maximum file size.** It checks only a 3 byte minimum ([file.rs line 213][u-cli-min]), that the disk has 1.1 times the file free ([native.rs lines 1039-1060][u-disk]), and that the length fits a `usize` (§1). A search of ant-cli and ant-core at `db85c72` for other size checks found none on the file path.
- **So 10 GB and 100 GB are possible today as one file, with the native client (`ant`).** They are not possible through the browser kit, which refuses anything above 1 GB both when storing ([wasm_transport.rs lines 2962-2965][u-stage-cap]) and when opening or downloading ([wasm_transport.rs lines 2850-2853][u-read-cap]). The upstream fix for big browser downloads is still open ([PR #214][pr214]).
- **Cost forecast:** 10 GB is about 188 ANT plus about $0.44 of gas, about **$6.42** in all. 100 GB is about 1,843 ANT plus about $4.10 of gas, about **$63**. Today's two native 1 GB estimates, scaled up, bracket $5.04 to $10.18 and $49 to $100 (§2). Arweave quoted **$560** and **$5,603** for the same sizes today.
- **Time forecast** (native client, good uplink): storing 10 GB takes about 2 to 4 h (1.2 h to 7.6 h), and 100 GB about 18 to 40 h (12 h to 3.2 days). Reading back natively takes about 1.8 to 2.5 h for 10 GB and about 18 to 25 h for 100 GB. These are extrapolations from runs of 0.2 to 3.6 GB, and the upstream upload run allowed four times more stores in flight than the pinned client does (§2).
- **Tungsten test:** four free quote stages that this seat can run now, then four paid stages. Each paid stage needs your own hand or your written exception: 250 MB (about 5 ANT), 1 GB (about 20 ANT), 10 GB (about 188 ANT), 100 GB (about 1,843 ANT). The whole ladder comes to about 2,055 ANT, about $70 with gas.
- **Prognosis:** 250 MB, 1 GB and 10 GB will likely pass. 100 GB is the risky one. The client pays for every batch before it stores anything, so for the one to two days of storing, about 1,843 ANT of paid proofs live in one local checkpoint file.
- **One of ours to fix:** My Data accepts files up to 264 MB. From 247 MB up, though, the bridge would be quoted a merkle payment that it cannot finish (§1; found by reading the code, not run).

This was found by reading the code; it was not run.

**Verdict.** 10 GB and 100 GB can be stored on Autonomi today as one file, through the native client (the `ant` CLI or the ant-core library). They cannot go through the browser kit, our relay or My Data. The kit could only take them as 1 GB parts: 10 or 100 parts, untested, and 5 % (10 GB) to 7 % (100 GB) dearer. For 10 GB that is 2,560 billable leaves against 2,432; for 100 GB, 25,600 against 23,872 ([receipt][rcpt] `projections.*.browserKit.asParts`).

Price notes:

- A record's price does not depend on its size. It depends only on how full the quoting node is: 0.00390625 + 0.03515625 × (n / 6000)^2 ANT ([pricing.rs:28-41, 83][ap-price]). The client pays 3 × the median quote ([payment_policy.rs:9][u-mult]).
- Merkle bills per padded leaf, so a file just past a power of two pays up to double for its last batch ([merkle.rs:665-697][u-mpad]).
- Uploading 23,870 new records (each lands in a close group of 7 nodes, [ant-protocol chunk.rs:125][ap-chunk]; how many of them keep it is node behaviour, not read here) will itself fill nodes, which may raise their prices. By how much is UNVERIFIED.
- Today's live browser quotes: 1 MB = 0.28976 ANT and 10 MB = 0.40411 ANT, 4 records each. The 100 MB quote did not finish inside its 10 minute cap (20 of 28 records quoted) ([receipt][rcpt] `browserQuotes`). An earlier quote of 0.214479087890625 ANT for 4,146 B has no kept raw output: UNVERIFIED.

## 3. Tungsten test design

This is a live ladder whose pass/fail is computed from observations, modelled on the doxx harness ([tools/net-doxx/README.md:36-61][o-doxx]). Proposed home: `tools/ant-tungsten/`. Not built.

**Verdicts** use the same scale as doxx:

- **FAIL**: money or data came out wrong.
- **INCONCLUSIVE**: a control failed, the harness errored, an observation is missing, or the machine slept.
- **PASS_WITH_LIMITATIONS**: everything held, but a forecast missed.
- **PASS**: everything held.

The ladder stops at the first FAIL. This note also proposes that a failed tungsten test is not turned into integration work; no written doxx precedent for that was found in the repo (the doxx README records a live FAIL and its blocker, [README.md:10-26][o-doxx-status]), so the precedent is UNVERIFIED.

**Controls before every stage:**

- A free native read of a known public file: try_autonomi.mp4, address `7c4f61ed…bb78`, 56 chunks, with a sha256 match. This shows the client and the network are alive, so a dead probe can neither pass nor fail a stage.
- The client is built from `db85c72` (the kit's own source, 13 commits after the ant-cli-v0.3.9 tag, so not the v0.3.9 release binary), and its commit goes into the receipt. This box has v0.3.7, which lacks the 16-store bound on the merkle store path that `db85c72` has.
- Each stage uses fresh random bytes. Nothing can dedupe, so the price is honest, and nothing personal is published.

If Auto falls back to waves (criterion 2), P3 needs about 0.0019 to 0.0020 ETH and P4 about 0.019 to 0.020 ETH (§2), well above their caps, so a fallback halts the run part-paid, and it can finish only if more ETH arrives before the merkle proofs already paid expire (7 days).

**Pass criteria for every paid stage:**

1. **The money matches the chain.** The payment-vault transfers in the run's txs are read from Arbitrum One through a public RPC, not through the client. They must sum to the client's `storage_cost_atto` to the atto, and the gas must equal `gas_cost_wei`. Any mismatch is a FAIL.
2. **Tx count.** The number of payment txs equals the predicted batches: 1, 1, 10 and 94. A fallback to waves ([upload.rs:863][u-fallback], [:895][u-fallback2]) is PASS_WITH_LIMITATIONS.
3. **Price within the forecast.** The ANT paid falls inside §2's wide band, rounded outward: P1 3.8 to 8.1, P2 15.2 to 32.2, P3 144 to 306, P4 1,417 to 2,998 (about −23 % to +63 % of central). Outside the band is PASS_WITH_LIMITATIONS, because the forecast was wrong. Paying above the cap cannot happen; an attempt is a FAIL.
4. **Everything stored.** `chunks_stored` = `total_chunks` = the predicted records, `chunks_failed` = 0, and the exit code is 0.
5. **Read back.** A fresh native download into an empty folder has the source's sha256. P2 is also read through the browser kit and the relay. For P3 and P4 the relay read is recorded, but it cannot fail the stage, because its stream cannot resume.
6. **No stranded payment.** On success the client deletes its checkpoint ([native.rs:2947][u-resume]). A partial run must finish on rerun with zero new payment txs, within 7 days of the first payment. Anything paid but not stored at the end is a FAIL.
7. **Time.** The run finishes inside §2's slow tail (P2 47 min, P3 7.6 h, P4 3.2 days). Past the tail is PASS_WITH_LIMITATIONS. Past 7 days is a FAIL, because the proofs expire ([upload_state.rs:58-66, :87-93][u-fresh]).
8. **Resources recorded:** phase timings (encryption, the pre-payment re-read, pool quotes and payment per batch, storing), peak RSS, spill and checkpoint sizes, uplink bytes, and the retries histogram. Running out of memory or disk is a FAIL.
9. **P3 only, the stop drill.** Stop the run once while it is storing, then rerun the same file. The client finds its checkpoint by the file's record set ([native.rs:2901-2947][u-resume]), and it must finish with no new payment tx. This proves resume works before P4 puts about 1,843 ANT behind it.
