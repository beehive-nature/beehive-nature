# Public evidence. Informed consent.

Our first bDroP begins with a commitment to preserve what was observed without pretending it establishes more than it does.

> Preserve reproducible findings and measured results. Keep observed behavior separate from inferred consequences.

Public experimental code stays public. Findings follow the BNR Auditors issue protocol. This record is not a substitute protocol, a security certification, or an announcement that upstream issues have been filed.

## The experiment and its limits

> RB01 is an experimental benchmark adapter. Its functional and performance results do not establish suitability for sensitive data or production use.

RB01 is the Swanky circuit-PSI benchmark lane associated with BNR PR #372. PSI means private set intersection: the experiment compares inputs using a privacy-oriented protocol. A measured result from that experiment is not, by itself, evidence that the complete composition is secure. [S1]

## Observation is not inference

> The constructors initialize separate RNG consumers from the same seed. The reporting seat observed overlapping streams within each party and matches between some generated bytes and protocol traffic. The security consequences remain unverified.

**Item 6's security impact remains UNVERIFIED unless further evidence establishes it.**

The constructor pattern is visible in the pinned `PsiGarbler::new` and `PsiEvaluator::new` source: each initializes a consumer for the garbling machinery and a retained consumer from the same supplied seed. [S2, S3]

The execution observations belong to the reporting seat. “Within each party” does not mean that the two parties share a seed with each other. This edition makes no claim of demonstrated input disclosure, secret recovery, or exploitation.

## Informed consent always with an operation

> Informed consent always with an operation.

Before execution, disclose the operation's purpose, data handling and recipients, established results, unresolved risks, resource and cost bounds, and reversibility. Execution must remain within the authorized scope. Existing bounded authorization can cover repeated operations; a material expansion must not happen silently.

Consent to an experiment is not consent to every future use of its data. Consent also does not transform **UNVERIFIED** into **PROVEN**, replace another participant's consent, or establish production suitability.

Public experimental code and public evidence do not require publishing real private inputs, credentials, or session secrets. This record contains the supplied synthetic-experiment synopsis, not anyone's private dataset.

## Eternal means preserving the record and its corrections

For this bDroP, “eternal” names the continuity commitment: preserve the original edition, identify its exact bytes, and link later corrections and new evidence rather than silently replacing what was said.

BNR's existing Hive-debut document already calls for a SHA-256 record of the text and for edits to fork rather than silently mutate. This edition follows that publication principle; it does not declare a new BNR issue protocol. [S4]

A blockchain publication can record that a statement was published. It does not prove that the statement is true. Long-term availability remains a preservation responsibility, not a guarantee supplied by the word “eternal.”

Future evidence may strengthen, narrow, or refute today's interpretation. Preserve that change as part of the public record. Until supporting evidence establishes item 6's security consequences, its impact remains **UNVERIFIED**.

**Public code. Public evidence. Explicit uncertainty. Execution within informed consent.**
