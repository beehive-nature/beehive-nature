Artwork, files and how it works

The original artwork was made by LoVis and his mother. This study preserves the selected original image inside 180 traced cells. The depth and motion are new interpretations.

Expressions are chosen by you. “Shared rhythm” describes the rings in this artwork; it does not connect to another device. This study does not send a gift, mint a token or change an existing inscription.

Your device’s reduced-motion preference keeps the vector artwork still. The video is a separate optional six-second film. Color meanings and the original artwork stay the same in every view.
