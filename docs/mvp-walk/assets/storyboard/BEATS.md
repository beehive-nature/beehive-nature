No motion picture was produced in this lane. The review board plays these beats as a clickable storyboard with static frames, readable captions, pause/replay, and a reduced-motion static sequence.

**Shared action:** a newcomer strings a short word, two people complete the founder-accepted PLUR continuity, the gift leaves the giver’s arm.  
**Shared destination:** `surfaces/kandi.html` (make / receive). Home is only for the separate grand-opening introduction.

## Production notes (not VO)

Two people throughout. Do not dissolve between unrelated signs.

1. **Peace:** V-sign fingertips touch.
2. **Love:** each person makes half a heart; index fingertips and thumb tips meet.
3. **Unity:** fingers move into each other’s gaps and interlock. Not prayer hands. Not a business handshake.
4. **Respect:** keep that clasp. The bracelet slides from the giver’s wrist, over the joined hands, onto the receiver’s wrist. The giver ends bare. The receiver wears the received piece beside their original one.

Software handoff is a different picture: the page copies a `KND1|…` line to the clipboard (or offers selectable text). A person carries that line. Copying is not delivery. A checksum is not ownership.
