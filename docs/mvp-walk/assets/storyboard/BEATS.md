## Production notes (founder canon — not VO, not filmed)

Two people throughout. Do not dissolve between unrelated signs. Do not treat the four-panel still as continuous film.

1. **Peace:** V-sign fingertips touch.
2. **Love:** touching fingertips. Each person makes half a heart; index fingertips and thumb tips meet.
3. **Unity:** interlocked fingers. Fingers move into each other’s gaps and lock. Not prayer hands. Not a business handshake.
4. **Respect:** bracelet travels giver wrist → receiver wrist while fingers stay locked. The giver ends bare. The receiver wears the received piece beside their original one.

Software handoff is a different picture: the page copies a `KND1|…` line to the clipboard (or offers selectable text). A person carries that line. Copying is not delivery. A checksum is not ownership.
