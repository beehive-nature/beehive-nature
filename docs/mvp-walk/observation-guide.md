# Newcomer observation guide — kandi gift loop

Canonical combined sheet: [`newcomer-observation-scorecard.md`](newcomer-observation-scorecard.md).

**Purpose:** watch whether a willing newcomer can make a bracelet and complete a handoff without being taught the clicks. This is an engineering observation sheet, not a statistical guarantee.

**Neutral goal (read this, then stop talking):**

> “Please make a little color and give it to someone. Use this page. I will not hint unless you ask to stop.”

## Identifiers

Anonymous **attempt IDs** (`A1`…`A5`). A real two-person handoff also gets a **pair ID** (`P1`). A second agent window is not a pair.

Use **Not observed** / **Not applicable** when a measure does not apply.

## Two different clocks

Do not share one “completed handoff” label for solo and paired attempts.

1. **Time to first bracelet** — piece visibly on an arm.
2. **Time to local handoff completion** — giver finished the local give. Solo attempts stop here. This is not a receiver Keep.
3. **Time to receiver Keep** — second clock, pairs only. Preview then Keep on the receiver’s device.

**Do not write either completion time for a decline.**

## Decline and the attempted-handoff denominator

Only declines **before starting** are excluded from the attempted-handoff denominator. A person who starts the give and then stops stays in that denominator; they still get no completion time.

## Distinctions

| Observed | Count as |
|---|---|
| Giver finishes the local give without hints | Unassisted local handoff |
| Receiver Keep on their own device | Observed receiver Keep (own clock) |
| Stop before starting the give | Decline before starting — exclude from attempted-handoff denominator |
| Start the give, then stop | Decline after starting — stays in that denominator |
| You clicked Keep in a fixture | Not a human observation |

## After

Fill one attempt-ID row on `observation-scorecard.md` or the combined sheet. No private payloads.
