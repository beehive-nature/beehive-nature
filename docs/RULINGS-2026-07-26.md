# RULINGS — 2026-07-26
### Founder decisions, with the recommended conditions attached. Citable by all seats.

---

## R1 — Lite accounts: APPROVED, identity only

Founder: yes, with recommended safeguards. The safeguards:

1. **Lite accounts carry identity and attribution, never value.** Attribution
   lives in `custom_json` payloads under the BNR community account (A24 limits:
   32-char id, 8KB). No b-token balance, no asset of any kind, attaches to a
   lite identity.
2. **Value requires graduation.** The moment a lite user would hold anything —
   b, HIVE, credits — they graduate to a real account (Options 1 or 3 in
   `ONBOARDING-OPTIONS.md`) first, and attribution migrates with them.
   Graduation is the fence crossing, done by the user acquiring their own keys.
3. **Attribution records are exportable from day one.** A lite user who leaves
   must be able to take their history. This keeps the lite tier inside the
   credible-exit posture rather than becoming a soft lock-in.
4. **Standing review trigger:** any feature proposal that would attach value to
   lite identities returns to the founder for a new ruling. This ruling covers
   identity only.

Rationale: custody of identity is reversible and exportable; custody of value
is neither. The line between them is the bACCORD fence, drawn where it can be
enforced mechanically (no balance fields on lite records) rather than by policy.

## R2 — Funnel go-order: as recommended

Build order confirmed:

1. **Keychain auth** — serves BYO users and externally-created accounts
   (Options 3+4) immediately; smallest surface, no treasury involvement.
2. **Lite-account attribution schema** — under R1's constraints.
3. **Hopper-routed `account_create`** — Option 1, reusing D-06 plumbing, with
   the A43 pre-flight fee read.

ACT path (Option 2) stays dormant with its ~6.5k HP auto-revisit trigger.

## R3 — BNRoSE default onboard: intermediate+, $10 self-custodial

Founder direction: the first default onboard targets intermediate-to-advanced
crypto users, who should be able to load up with ~$10 of whatever crypto they
hold and receive enough b and other assets to have a real runway on the surface.

Accepted with one structural condition, consistent with the standing fence
ruling: **BNR is never in the flow of funds.** The user executes every
conversion leg with their own wallets along documented routes; BNRoSE watches,
verifies arrival, and provisions. The $10 buys the user *their own* on-chain
assets; b is **granted by the treasury on completion, not sold** — a b-for-crypto
sale would make the onboard a token sale, which is a different regulatory
object entirely. (Token distribution has its own legal surface even as a grant —
flagged in the spec's open items; not legal advice, and worth a real lawyer
before public launch.)

Spec: `SPEC-BNROSE-ONBOARD.md`.
