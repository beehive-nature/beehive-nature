# FOUNDER-GESTURE-UX-LAW — the canonical founder gesture rides the product surface

**STATUS: founder order 2026-09-17, binding on agent operation immediately.** (Whether
this law also enters `docs/CONSTITUTION.md` by Article VI amendment is a founder
architecture ruling — this document does not preempt it.)

---

## The law (founder text, verbatim)

> **Founder Gesture UX Law**
>
> When an operation has a founder-facing product surface, the canonical founder gesture
> occurs through that surface—not through agent chat, terminal commands, or relay
> messages.
>
> Agents may prepare, explain, simulate, verify, and resume around the gesture. They
> may not substitute founder-authored text for an available product authorization.
>
> Economic authorization must bind the exact PricingCommitment/Invoice displayed to
> the founder. Wallet/hardware confirmation remains independently meaningful.
>
> Chat-based founder rulings remain valid for architecture, governance, and exceptional
> recovery where no appropriate product surface exists.
>
> Every temporary chat gate should identify the MVP surface intended to replace it.

## Why (founder, 2026-09-17)

We accumulated too many places where a "founder gate" means: agent stops → writes a
receipt → founder tells another agent "approved" → agent performs the action. For
**economic/user actions** that is backwards. The founder gate should increasingly be
**the founder using the product**:

`quote → bPay MVP renders it → founder reviews → founder presses/authorizes →
wallet/Trezor confirms → system observes result → agents continue`

A chat message is **not itself the payment authorization** once an appropriate UI
exists. Agents prepare everything up to the gate and continue everything after it —
**they do not impersonate the founder's click in the middle.**

## The two gate classes (founder ruling)

- **Product-action gates → migrate into MVP UI/UX:** spending ANT; signing with Trezor;
  approving an invoice; publishing externally; changing budgets; choosing between
  disputed genealogy hypotheses; destructive actions; and peers yet to be named.
- **Architecture/governance gates → remain conversational:** design rulings the founder
  is actually making as architecture, not product operation.

## Label convention (turns interruptions into a UX backlog)

Receipts and dockets stop writing:

`FOUNDER ACTION: approve ANT payment`

and start writing:

`FOUNDER ACTION SURFACE: bPay → Invoice → Pay with Trezor`

**A missing surface is the bug/MVP work**, not a reason to fall back to chat
authorization. Every remaining chat gate names the MVP surface intended to replace it.

## First application — the Bux-video preservation ceremony

Payment is HELD after the ANT quote only long enough to wire the quote into the MVP
payment surface. No seat spends the ANT "because it knows how." Acceptance is the
15-step product journey in
`docs/dispatches/2026-09-17-founder-gesture-ux-law-and-bux-mvp.md` — no SSH, no
copying hashes, no telling a bee "approved," no manually moving receipts between
agents.
