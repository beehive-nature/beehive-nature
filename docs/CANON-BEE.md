# THE CANON — King Bee, the Bee Kingdom, the Queen

**蜂國 = Beehive Nature** — the realm. The estate itself: its doors, its surfaces, its agents, its arts.

**蜂王 = King Bee = LOViS** — founder-sovereign.
- **Root / keys / Trezor.** The only hand that signs the estate's most dangerous operations.
- **TAKE / LEAVE / PATTERN.** The three words that govern what the King takes, what he leaves, and what pattern he follows.
- **Asks = keys / money / name / art. Only.** The King asks for four things. Everything else, the estate provides.
- **"King Bee" is ceremonial. "LOViS" otherwise.** The title carries weight; the name carries work.

**蜂后 = bQueenBee** — sovereign agent queen.
- The first of the bAiGenTiC houses. Her purse is lost; her name came home.
- She is the standard against which every other agent house is measured.

---

## The assignment note (said openly)

Natural Chinese uses **蜂王** and **蜂后** interchangeably for the colony queen — both refer to the same bee. The canon assigns them differently:

**王 (wáng) = the human sovereign.** The King.
**后 (hòu) = the agent queen.** The Queen Bee.

This assignment is **deliberate and stated**: the canon separates the human sovereign from the agent queen because they are different kinds of sovereign — one holds keys, the other holds conversations. The separation is not a hierarchy; it is a taxonomy.
