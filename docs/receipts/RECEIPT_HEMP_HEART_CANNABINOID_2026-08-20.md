## 1 · The direct measurement — confirmed HULLED seed

**Food Standards Australia New Zealand, Survey of Low-THC Hemp Seed Foods, Stage 2
(July 2022)** — the only large dataset where *every* seed sample was visually confirmed
hulled by the testing laboratory. n=55 products, six ISO/IEC-17025 labs, LC-MS/MS and
UPLC-MS against ISO17034 reference standards.

| | min | median | mean | **max** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| total THC (Δ9 + 0.877×THCA) | 0.1 | **0.9** | 1.1 | **5.7 mg/kg** |
| total CBD (CBD + 0.877×CBDA) | 0.3 | **1.5** | 1.8 | **9.3 mg/kg** |

Median THCA+CBDA-family sum ≈ **2.4 mg/kg = 0.24 mg per 100 g**.
The claim of 1 g per 100 g is **~4,000× that median** and **~1,750× the single worst
hulled sample ever recorded in the survey.**
<https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/sites/default/files/science-data/surveillance/SiteAssets/Pages/Survey-of-low-THC-hemp-seed-foods/Low-THC-Hemp-Stage-2-report.pdf>

## 2 · The kernel itself, physically separated

**Ross, Mehmedic, Murphy & ElSohly 2000 (J Anal Toxicol, GC-MS)** dissected seeds into
testa and kernel. Fiber-type **kernels: <0.5 µg/g** Δ9-THC (<0.00005% w/w). ~90% of total
seed THC removed by a single chloroform wash — the cannabinoid sits on the *outside* of
the testa. **Kacargil et al. 2026 (Forensic Sci Int 381:112836)** is the only paper
quantifying **THCA directly** in seed: **0.01–1.18 µg/g, mean 0.60 µg/g** (0.00006% w/w).

## 3 · The mechanism — why this is biology, not processing luck

Cannabinoids are synthesised **exclusively in stalked glandular trichomes** on
inflorescences, bracts and leaves. **Trichomes are absent on seeds.** The seed has no
THCA-synthase-bearing tissue and no endogenous synthesis (Farinon et al. 2020, *Nutrients*;
Citti et al. 2019 — seeds are *"void of cannabinoids in the inside"*).

**Our own tree already said this.** The in-tree GRAS 765 material, 9/9 claims
verbatim-confirmed by the compile gate, quotes FDA: THC in dehulled hemp seed *"comes
from contact between the seeds and cannabinoid-containing resins from other plant
parts."* Dehulling **removes** the contaminated substrate — hearts are the fraction the
contamination was taken *off* of.

**At 1% THCA, hemp hearts would be ~2,500× the FDA-reviewed specification** — about

## 5 · THE STEELMAN — where the founder IS right

**1–2% total cannabinoids is a real and common figure — for the wrong material.** It is
**plant-material territory**: it brackets the 0.2%/0.3% Δ9-THC legal definitions applied
to flowering tops, and the cannabinoid content of hemp leaf and low-grade biomass.
And **"hemp oil" tinctures — hemp EXTRACT diluted into hemp seed oil — routinely run
5–30% CBD by weight** (Germany's national survey found a hemp food supplement at
3,400 mg/kg THC = 0.34%). Those are *added extracts*, not seed content.

So the number is not invented; it is **correct for extract and plant material, and
misapplied to the dehulled kernel.** The distinction is the single most consequential
one in this whole food category, and it cuts in the project's favour: hearts are a
**food**, which is exactly why the presentation law (lead with nutrition, never cannabis)
is the right call and is now doubly supported.
