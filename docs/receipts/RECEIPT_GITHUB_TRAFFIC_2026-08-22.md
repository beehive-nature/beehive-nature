## 7 · Honest limits

- Traffic API only shows 14 days of data; longer trends are not yet visible.
- Cloners ≠ users ≠ contributors — they are developers who took the code, which is
  the strongest pre-engagement signal available, but not adoption.
- The referrer data undercounts Discord-sourced traffic (Discord links often render
  as `github.com` direct after redirect).
- Stars are low relative to cloners — typical for infrastructure projects where
  developers clone to evaluate, star only after sustained use.
