## 1 · THE RULING, OPERATIONALIZED

BiGen's evidence classes gain a terminal state: **VOIDED — contamination proven.**
A study whose material is shown to carry adulterants (petrochemical residues,
undeclared synthetics, heavy-metal loads above the receipted floor) is publicly voided,
and the proof publishes **beside** the high-confidence lane — the void is an exhibit,
not a deletion.

## 2 · DESIGN LAWS ATTACHED — Seat 3 draft, each strikeable by one founder line

1. **Voiding requires a receipted assay, never suspicion.** The void's exhibit carries
   the contamination evidence at the same standard the COA spec demands of ourselves:
   named analytes, LOQ floors, chain of custody. An accusation without an assay is
   itself the kind of claim BiGen refuses.
2. **Adulterated ≠ refuted.** A voided study's conclusions become **UNGRADED**, not
   disproven. Contamination destroys the evidence; it does not invert the finding.
   Rendering a void as a refutation would be the category collapse this house has now
   caught five times, applied to epistemics.
3. **The rule is symmetric, and it already has teeth pointed at us.** `SPEC-CB4-ASSAY-1`
   pre-fills `manufacturer_funded: true` on our own commissioned assay. The identical
   void applies to our own lots and our own studies: if our material fails a
   contamination screen, the void publishes with the same prominence. The negative
   control stands — if the flag ever renders softer on the study we paid for, the
   detector is broken.
4. **Founder-hands for naming names.** A void that names a third party's study is
   outward-facing content. The lane composes the exhibit; the founder publishes it —
   same boundary as bIQ and every external instrument.

## 3 · ROUTING

BiGen design is the research/evidence lane's artefact — this ruling routes there for
absorption into the grading grammar (a `VOIDED` verdict class beside GRADE tiers).
The festival lane inherits it automatically: any commercial data product carries the
same contamination screens its own COA spec already mandates.
