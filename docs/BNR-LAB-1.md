# BNR-LAB-1 — Charter: the Hardware Lab
### "Tom's Hardware for the sovereign age, reviewed under stage lights" · seed for raid ratification

## What it is

A review lab and publication inside the onboarding education surface. Manufacturers of open hardware send devices; the BNR surgical code team tests them two ways and publishes both, always together:

- **THE SCIENCE (AI + code team):** automated, reproducible bench runs. Every number regenerable from a published harness — FIDO2/PRF conformance (fido2-tests), optical throughput under real conditions (UR/bComb capture rates at 60 Hz screens, hand tremor, sunlight), air-gap integrity (RF silence verification), battery under signing load, firmware reproducible-build check, license audit. The benchmark harness is open source; a vendor who disputes a number can run it themselves. Receipts, not adjectives.
- **THE ART (the founder):** lived, human review. Does it survive a festival night? Does the ceremony feel sovereign or bureaucratic? Would you hand one to your mother, your raver friend, your skeptical uncle? Style is data — a device nobody wants to touch protects nobody.

A review ships only when both halves exist. Science without art is a spec sheet; art without science is an ad.

## The ethics law (the part that makes it credible for 1,000 years)

1. **Review units are disclosed on every review, always.** "Unit provided by manufacturer, unretained/retained-for-regression" printed at the top. Gifts never buy verdicts; the disclosure is what proves it.
2. **No paid placement, no affiliate links, ever.** Links go direct to manufacturer and source repo — the same links a stranger would find. The lab is funded by the community it serves, not the vendors it reviews.
3. **Only open tech gets a comb.** Closed devices are not reviewed — they're listed once in the excluded index with the reason named. We do not generate marketing for locked boxes by fighting with them.
4. **Negative results publish.** A device that fails the bench fails in public, with the data, and the vendor gets right-of-reply appended verbatim. (Same doctrine as the security-events page: disclosure is health.)
5. **Regression retesting.** Firmware updates re-run the bench; scores are versioned to firmware, never evergreen.

## The Comb Score

Six axes, one hexagon — rendered as a bComb-style hex with six cells, each lit 0–6:
**⬡ Openness** (firmware/hardware/SE, license quality) · **⬡ Air-gap** (modalities, UR interop) · **⬡ Custody law** (phrase floor, no exfil paths, disclosure honesty) · **⬡ Build** (hardware quality, supply chain, repairability) · **⬡ Art** (ceremony feel, festival survivability — founder's cell, not computable) · **⬡ Ecosystem** (BNR-stack fit: FIDO2/PRF rung, multisig leg, bSAFE forkability).
No overall number — a hexagon is the honest shape; the sixth cell belongs to a human.

## Presence in onboarding

The lab lives as page 4 of the education surface (guide · security · build · **lab**). Manufacturers reviewed there get their guide-page entry annotated "⬡ bench-tested" — the presence they earn by shipping a unit and surviving the harness.

## Complementary gear (the section, not reviews)

Hardware and software that completes a sovereign kit without being a wallet: metal backup plates, Faraday sleeves, NIR-capable cameras and Pi kits (builder rung), LoRa/mesh radios (bMeshNetWork), NFC readers, open wearable dev kits — and the FOSS software shelf (Sparrow, Nunchuk, the BNR surfaces themselves). Curated by the same law: open, direct links, no affiliates.

## The founding case study

The lab's ethics law is not theoretical — 2026 handed the industry its proof. The Coldcard RNG failure (a 2021 regression that silently swapped hardware entropy for a predictable generator, disclosed by Block in July 2026 after five years and active exploitation) happened inside a review culture where researchers were called terrorists, reproducibility auditors were threatened with lawsuits, and one founder's reputation stood in for verification — what Zach Herbert named *epistemic capture*: a community outsourcing its judgment to a trusted authority until the authority becomes the evidence. Every clause above is the negation of one mechanism in that story: units disclosed (no quiet favor economy), negative results with right-of-reply (technical disputes stay technical), only-open-gets-a-comb (review must be *possible*), scores versioned to firmware (no evergreen halo), and the Art cell held by a named human (charisma gets one cell, never six). The lab exists so that when a BNR device fails, it fails in public, in year one, on our own bench first. Do not trust the lab either — every bench number regenerates from the open harness. Verify.
