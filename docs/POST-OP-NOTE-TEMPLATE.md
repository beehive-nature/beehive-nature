# POST-OP NOTE — RATIFIED TEMPLATE
### Standing for every operation on code, doc, or media

**Authority:** Seat 0 (King Bee), ratified 2026-08-08. **Filed by:** Cowork.
**Applies to:** every operation on code, doc, or media. Not optional, not per-seat.

---

## Why this exists, in one line

**A changelog says what changed. A post-op note says what happened, what was found, what
went wrong, and what the next operator must know.**

It is **written for a later surgeon who was not present.** That reader is the test: if they
could not safely pick up the case from your note alone, the note is not finished.

## The eight fields — all eight, every time

| # | Field | What it must carry |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **PRE-OP STATE** | The state as found. Verifiable — shas, versions, counts, not impressions |
| 2 | **POST-OP STATE** | The state as left. Same standard |
| 3 | **PROCEDURE PERFORMED** | What was actually done, in order |
| 4 | **SEATS PRESENT** | Who was in the operation — **carries authorship per LAW 8c** |
| 5 | **FINDINGS** | What was discovered, including what was expected and absent |
| 6 | **SPECIMENS** | Evidence taken — **carries crate + ref per LAW 8a** |
| 7 | **COMPLICATIONS** | What went wrong. **Recorded, never softened** |
| 8 | **DISPOSITION** | What the next operator must know, do, or avoid |

An empty field is stated as empty (`COMPLICATIONS: none`), never omitted. **An omitted field
reads as "not considered," which is a different and worse claim than "none."**

## BNRoSe style layer — how the fields are written

- **Clinical register.** No salesmanship. No hedging that hides a defect.
- **VERIFIED / REPORTED / UNVERIFIED on every factual claim.** Not a decoration — a reader
  must be able to tell what you *checked* from what you were *told* from what you *assumed*.
- **Fence-holds stated as fence-holds.** A held decision is written as held, never quietly
  resolved in the prose.
- **No security language stronger than "sound by construction / isolated by design."**
- **SPECIMENS carry crate + ref (8a). SEATS PRESENT carries authorship (8c).**

## Two failure modes this template forbids

1. **The clean note.** A note with no COMPLICATIONS because they were smoothed out in the
   writing. If nothing went wrong, say so; if something did, it belongs in field 7 whether
   or not it was fixed. **A defect that gets hedged into invisibility is worse than an
   unrecorded one, because it reads as cleared.**
2. **The changelog in disguise.** Fields 1–3 filled, 4–8 thin. That is a diff with headings.
   The value is concentrated in **FINDINGS, COMPLICATIONS, and DISPOSITION** — the three
   fields a changelog cannot produce.

## Naming variance is not ambiguity

**The founder will address seats by whatever name suits the moment. Do not ask which seat is
meant.** Naming variance carries no information about assignment; treat the instruction on
its content. This is explicitly *not* in tension with LAW 8c — 8c governs **provenance of
claims** (who said a thing), not **modes of address** (what a seat is called).

## Relationship to LAW 8f

A post-op note is an instance of **"summary that is true alone, depth that is reachable."**
DISPOSITION is the kernel — sufficient alone for the next operator to act correctly.
FINDINGS and SPECIMENS are the reachable depth. A DISPOSITION that only makes sense after
reading SPECIMENS is a teaser, not a disposition.
