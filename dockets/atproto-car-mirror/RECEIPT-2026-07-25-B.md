The packet orders `git config format.signOff true` (or `-s`) "so subsequent
commits carry the trailer." **ORDERS-1 v0.8 §DCO — verified current in the
tree at execution time — says the opposite for this seat**, verbatim:

> "Machine seats never emit `Signed-off-by:` under their own identity — a

> `Signed-off-by: loVis waTer <loviswater44@gmail.com>` **only** to merges

A blanket `format.signOff` under this repo's committer identity would make
this machine seat stamp the **founder's** certification on every commit —
precisely what §DCO scopes to founder-gated merges only, and the clause
exists because three machine sign-offs (`3f920e9`, `e2d1551`, `07b0987`)
were ruled the precedent it **ends**. The three unsigned atmirror commits
(`fc17330`, `0b770b9`, `1474d57`) are therefore §DCO-compliant as they
stand, with seat credit carried by `Co-Authored-By:` — the channel §DCO
names. The 14 founder-signed ledger commits alongside them are the founder
certifying the founder's own work: also exactly per law.

**The contradiction to resolve (founder's, one line):** dispatch CC-4 and
ledger `a44a2ed` treat the absence as a *provenance gap*; ORDERS §DCO
defines it as the *lawful state*. If machine-lane commits should carry a
DCO trailer going forward, §DCO needs the amendment saying under whose
identity and what certification a machine commit makes; until that diff
lands, this seat does not configure sign-off. Not retroactive either way
(§DCO: "fixed forward, never retroactively").
