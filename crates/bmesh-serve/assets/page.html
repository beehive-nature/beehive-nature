🐘 POSTGRES — the honest absence, by design

Not armed in this build. postgres compiles only with --features postgres — its polar strength is many concurrent writers and rich queries at scale, which is not this lane's demand. When a lane's function demands it (the bounty board's queue, metering at fleet scale), it is armed deliberately and the same Journal contract serves it. sqlite carries the default everywhere else: one file, embedded, git-friendly, zero administration.
