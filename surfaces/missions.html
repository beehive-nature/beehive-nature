Every operation here is a supervised mission. You approve the mission and its spending ceiling; the team works in a Buzz room; a separate reviewer verifies; every meaningful action leaves a receipt. Nothing is spent above the ceiling, no scope grows silently, and a stop from you halts everything.

The LOVErnment runs care as governance — mīlestība ir karalis, love is king. Missions ride the same floor: the founder holds the faders (approval, ceiling, stop), the crew plays the set, and every act lands in the ledger so the morning after still tells the truth.

Off-chain first rail: proposals and hash-chained receipts with testnet accounting, local-first over a deterministic seed. The Buzz relay wire and the ERC20i treasury are later rails — named, not claimed. Verify the chain; trust nothing unlabeled.

Named by the code that runs it, per the estate's honesty law.

storage

buzz wire
UNWIRED — the relay event mapping is designed (proposal + receipt envelopes in community rooms), not built; no relay connection exists on this page
money
testnet accounting — USD-denominated fixture figures; no token, wallet, or chain is touched from this surface
never
silent scope growth · spend above the approved ceiling · secrets exposure · unverified claims published · irreversible deploys
