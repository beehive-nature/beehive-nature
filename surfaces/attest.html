Every page of this estate already speaks your language — but by a machine's hand, and it says so honestly with a little ⚙ beside every line. We are looking for one person per tongue, one evening each, to read those lines and sign them. When the last signature lands, this becomes something rare: an estate whose every language was vouched for by a person who lives in it.
the attestation campaign: ~197 wayfinding strings × every docked tongue, all machine-drafted and labeled ⚙ on their face. attestation is review, not retranslation — one focused evening per language turns its ⚙ to ✓ estate-wide, your name (or your chosen silence) on the record forever. eighteen strangers, one tongue, one evening, history.
Campaign mechanics: the corpus (lang-corpus.json) carries _meta.attested, empty by design until humans sign. An attestation is a PR: corrected lines via the DOCK workshop's merge verb + an attested entry {by|silence, date}. The moment it merges, the estate-wide picker renders ✓ for that tongue instead of ⚙ — the campaign's progress is enforced by the renderer, not claimed by a page. Effort, measured: review at ~15–25 s/string ≈ 1–2 h/tongue; the full set ≈ 1–2 human-hours per tongue, perfectly parallel.

HOW — one evening, four steps, no account

1 · open the DOCK workshop, pick 🔀 merge, choose your tongue — every line prefills beside its English.
2 · read; fix what a native ear rejects (the standing law, the founder's words: "make all appropriate to the culture / language / value / history / future"). Most lines will need nothing — say so by leaving them.
3 · compose the fragment, and add your attested entry: {"attested":{"YOUR-CODE":{"by":"your name — or 'silence'","date":"YYYY-MM-DD"}}}
4 · open a PR against surfaces/lang-corpus.json (or hand the fragment to any hive member on any rail). The moment it merges, every page in the estate switches your tongue's ⚙ to ✓ — the renderer enforces your signature, forever, in the git record.
The laws that protect you and everyone after you: a machine draft never finalizes a consent (ceremony strings are excluded from this campaign by law and await their own stricter process) · names are names, never translated · your withdrawal, should you ever want it, is honored with history kept — the dock forgets nothing.
