    1 · THE FAT ASSEMBLY LINE — upstream medicine, stage by stage, animated

    six stages arrive in causal order. the green dots mark the essential — the molecules that must arrive from outside; nothing downstream can replace them. the button at the end runs the whole line backwards: the reversal hypothesis, with its grade appearing on every stage it crosses.
    STAGE 1 · THE TWO BRICKS — essential, cannot be synthesized
    linoleic acid · n-6 (LA)
    DRI AI row exists (the Hexagon) — the n-6 headwater
    α-linolenic acid · n-3 (ALA)
    DRI AI row exists — the n-3 headwater. no authoritative n-6:n-3 target exists; a page printing one invents it
    100 g hemp hearts = 27.4 g LA + 8.7 g ALA — both essential bricks, one seed, ≈3.2:1 (USDA FDC 170148)
    STAGE 2 · THE BODY'S WORKSHOP — enzymes, and honestly rate-limited
    desaturase and elongase enzymes refit the bricks into signaling fats — LA toward arachidonic acid, ALA toward EPA/DHA
    the honest brake, carried with the chemistry: conversion is rate-limited and inefficient — lowering dietary LA alone does not simply re-set arachidonic acid or the endocannabinoids built from it (Lipids 2023). the workshop exists; it is not a firehose.
    STAGE 3 · TWO DIALECTS OF ONE LANGUAGE — the body's own cannabinoids
    n-6 dialect: anandamide + 2-AG
    your endocannabinoids are arachidonoyl-class molecules — fat-derived cannabinoids your body makes itself (JBC 2025). anandamide is named for ananda — bliss — because that is what the molecule does to CB1
    n-3 dialect: omega-3 endocannabinoids
    the omega-3 family writes its own endocannabinoid class — modulating T-cell activity, the immune seam (JBC 2023)
    the sentence the industry never says: cannabinoid receptors did not evolve for plants — plants happened to speak the language your fats built
    STAGE 4 · THE INTERFACE — where the language is heard
    CB1 — central
    Δ9-THC's unique cell: partial agonist, BBB-crossing (Guzmán 2003). CBD is a negative allosteric modulator here — CB1-negative without antagonism (Mol Pharmacol 2022)
    CB2 — the peripheral-class receptor
    the CB2 half of the agonist pharmacology (eCAM 2022) — the immune-seam receptor where the n-3 dialect's T-cell modulation lands. the severity ladder (10:1 · 5:1 · 3:1) reads on these two axes
    PRESENTATION · THE MADE DISEASE
    ACiD — Artificial Chronic Inflammatory Disease
    the inflammatory tone, run chronically by artificial inputs — the ultra-processed class, petrochemical adulterants (the exact material class the contamination-void law strikes from the literature). made, therefore in principle unmakeable — remove the artificial, supply the essential. 🧪 the unmaking is the graded hypothesis
    STAGE 5 · THE LONG HORIZON — machinery, then the readout
    TERT → telomerase → telomere maintenance — universal eukaryotic biology (Greider & Blackburn 1985)
    THE FLAGSHIP STUDY — founder-designated, and the one measurement where this page's two arms meet: Hussein et al. 2014 (Alexandria J Med, 10.1016/j.ajme.2014.02.003 — in vivo, mouse, hepatic TERT mRNA: 116.13 → 2.29 / 3.08 / 9.60 copies after a ~2:1 THC-dominant cannabis extract; in-tree as BiGen's card C). the flagship carries its flags, as flagships must in this house: mRNA was measured, telomerase activity was inferred — no TRAP assay; unregistered; and a THC-dominant extract is the central-CB1-agonist lane, so its result does not transfer to the CB1-negative lane this page feeds — the inverse question (the n-3/CB1-negative dialect and TERT in normal cells) is UNTESTED, and that open cell is the founder's thesis wearing its honest name. and the association that closes the epic arc: higher marine omega-3 levels, slower telomeric aging — CHD patients, co-authored by Blackburn herself (JAMA 2010; association class — one randomized diet→telomere trial exists, Nutrients 2025). the assay brake ships beside it: qPCR telomere measurement's own review says reproducibility is still being standardized (Lin 2019) — single timepoints are hypothesis-grade. 🧪 nutrition preserving the readout = the thesis, pre-registered
