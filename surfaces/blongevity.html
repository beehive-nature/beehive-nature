UPSTREAM · SUBSTRATE
omega fats — the only two essential fatty acids
linoleic acid (n-6) and α-linolenic acid (n-3) cannot be synthesized — DRI AI rows exist for both (the Hexagon). downstream of them, the endocannabinoids themselves are fatty-acid derivatives — anandamide and 2-AG are arachidonoyl-class substrates (JBC 2025): the omega lane feeds the receptor lane's own signal molecules. no authoritative n-6:n-3 target exists — a page printing one invents it (Hexagon §2).
INTERFACE · RECEPTORS
CB1 · CB2 — the two-axis interface
the functional-naming settlement's axes: CB1 central (Δ9-THC's unique cell — partial agonist, BBB-crossing) · CB2 the peripheral-class receptor (the CB2 half of the agonist pharmacology, eCAM 2022; receptor expression across tissues including immune cells, Guzmán 2003). CBD is a negative allosteric modulator of CB1 — CB1-negative without antagonism (Mol Pharmacol 2022). the severity ladder (10:1 · 5:1 · 3:1) reads on these axes.
PROCESS
chronic inflammation
the process class between the interface and the disease — the tier-3 endpoint the festival telemetry lane measures (CRP-class markers under consent, never surveillance).
PRESENTATION
ACiD — Artificial Chronic Inflammatory Disease
the founder's definition, on the face: the burden is made — artificial inputs (the ultra-processed class, petrochemical adulterants and residues — the exact material class the contamination-void law strikes from the literature). a made disease is unmakeable: remove the artificial, supply the essential. 🧪 the unmaking is the graded hypothesis
MACHINERY
TERT → telomerase → telomere maintenance
telomere biology is universal eukaryotic physiology — the telomerase discovery line (Greider & Blackburn 1985). maintenance machinery read out over years, not meals. 🧪 attrition as ACiD's long-run cost
READOUT · LONG HORIZON
telomere length — an association-class readout
measurable, gradable, slow: the qPCR assay's own review says reproducibility is still being standardized (Lin 2019) — single timepoints are hypothesis-grade. the RCT class exists: a moderately high-protein diet moved telomere length in a randomized trial (Nutrients 2025). 🧪 nutrition preserving the readout = the thesis, pre-registered
