    index · rails · identity

    index {addr:{scheme,address}, name, size, purpose, keyref, ts} in this browser's IndexedDB — this device is the truth.
    storage is an adapter, never this page: each rail runs in its own dedicated Web Worker behind SPEC-ADAPTER-CONTRACT-1, and this page holds the key, the signature, the retry and the index — nothing else.
    you choose a purpose; the page picks a rail whose own declared terms answer it. Those terms — deletable? who reads? how long? who paid? — come from the rail's describe, and are printed on every file above. This page does not know them.
    a keep-it-here purpose is never routed to a rail that declares any network, and the two rails that serve those purposes contain no network primitive at all. What they hold is AES-GCM ciphertext; the key stays in this page, so there is no key on that side to hold.
    a file kept forever is paid by you, from your own wallet. This page cannot take that payment yet: it refuses every price by name and asks your wallet for nothing. It never holds, derives or stores a wallet key.
    identity = a key generated once in this browser and kept here. No login, no session.
