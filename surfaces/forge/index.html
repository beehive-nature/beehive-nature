    the whole forge

    🐜 the bBuzz forge ⚙

    the creation wing of the bBuzz studio — sound and sight, artist-first. Describe what you want, the harness builds it, you see and hear it, then you fork it and make it yours. ⚙ everything the AI seat touches wears the gear badge; everything you forge from it is yours.

    what it is

    A forge for artists who want instruments, not presets. One loop drives both media: describe → build → preview → iterate → fork. The sound side compiles real audio plugins (CLAP, built on nih-plug); the sight side grows generative pieces from a seed. Nothing here is locked: every template opens to its full source, and the whole estate is a public Rust-and-web workspace you can read end to end.

    the teaching promise

    • No build without an explain block — the harness says what it chose and why, what each knob does, and suggests one small thing to try. A teacher, not a vending machine.
    • Practice loops — one change at a time, narrated: "you raised density — the lace tightened." A bad change is a finding, not an error.
    • Guidance that ends by pointing at the source — the lane succeeds when you outgrow it.
    the fork law: every piece can be forked with one gesture — roll the seed, copy the link — and the parent stays untouched, forever. The community's fork-tree is the gallery.

    the sound side

    BuzzGain ⚙ — the forge's first audio template: drive → tape saturation → trim → a hard-wired safety wrapper (soft-knee limiter, NaN/∞ silence, crash guard). Built as a real CLAP plugin on our own box, host-validated (29/44, identical to the reference implementation), with VST3 as a deliberate GPLv3-gated opt-in. The human ear-test in a DAW is the one receipt still owed — that one's on a human with headphones.
