      THE TWO PROTOCOLS, ONE SENTENCE EACH

      MAHA — the state protocol
      Executive Order 14212 (signed Feb 13 2025, published 90 FR 9833) establishes the President's Make America Healthy Again Commission, chaired by the Secretary of HHS. Its Assessment names four potential drivers of childhood chronic disease — poor diet first, then environmental chemicals, inactivity and stress, overmedicalization — and the Commission has moved on dyes, SNAP restrictions, pesticide scrutiny, and a September 2025 national strategy. [EO 14212 — Federal Register, the durable copy · whitehouse.gov · the Strategy — USDA release 0213.25, Sep 9 2025]
      bFood — the measurement protocol
      An open (AGPL) nutrition-instrument stack: a per-body reference-intake engine (the EER equations, DRI tables), a lot-level COA specification with an LOQ floor law, three display laws (NotMeasured ≠ 0 · never an undifferentiated "saturated fat" · never a "total" without its panel size), and an evidence ladder that refuses to let a plan masquerade as a finding. [the bFood Hexagon · the COA spec · the university that teaches it]

      THE SYMBIOSIS LANES — what each side brings, and the co-creations waiting

      ⚙ what MAHA uniquely holds

      Federal levers no open project can reach: labeling authority (the dye phase-out shows the reach), procurement (school food, military, SNAP experimentation — the June 2026 ruling itself named the lawful shape: "experimental projects"), and research direction at NIH/FDA scale. bipartisan receipts exist for the food lane — Senator Sanders called the stance on the food industry "exactly correct"; Governor Polis said he was "excited" — the co-creation lane is real politics, not wishful thinking.

      🔬 what bFood uniquely holds

      A working measurement grammar, free and open: the lot-COA specification (LOQ floor, ≥200-analyte pesticide screen, no-blank-cells), the three display laws, a per-body reference engine, a tiered evidence ladder, and a receipts-first publishing discipline in production on 34 public surfaces. nothing in it requires permission or budget to adopt — it is AGPL infrastructure looking for a mandate.

      🤝 five co-creations, ready to start

      1 · lot-level COA transparency — our spec as a voluntary federal standard: any food, any producer, one record shape, stranger-verifiable.
      2 · NotMeasured ≠ 0 as label law — a regulation-ready grammar for the difference between "absent from the panel" and "zero", the exact error class that misleads label readers.
      3 · fatty-acid chain-length disclosure — serves BOTH camps of the seed-oil fight: the reader sees the classes are different quantities before anyone tells them they behave differently. disclosure without assertion is the homeostatic move.
      4 · receipts-disciplined publishing — the mechanical answer to the citation failure both agentic eras keep hitting (ours too — see line 7): no claim ships without a fetchable receipt; failed fetches declared; corrections in place. the six-checks validator pattern is reusable by any agency.
      5 · SNAP experiments with the tier ladder — the court's own words invite experiments; the four-tier ladder (composed → self-reported → receipt-backed → assayed) is the honest grammar for them: it says what each result can and cannot claim.
