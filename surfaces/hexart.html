how this is built (the honest spec)

Everything runs in this page — no uploads, no accounts, no network. The default engine is a seeded value-noise field quantized onto the cells with an ordered comb dither; the same seed and parameters always regrow the same piece, and your hand edits ride inside the recipe as an overlay. The hexagon grid is the estate’s visual fingerprint: you can look at a piece and know it came out of the hive. Engines are replaceable adapters — a local model can grow the field one day without changing the recipe, the editor, or the export. The cell-grid representation is ready for animation, sprites, rotations and tilesets without a core rebuild; those are future doors, stated here so they are not silent.
