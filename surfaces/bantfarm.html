⛏ NODE OPERATIONS — where the farm actually runs

Your nodes run on your machine (the Autonomi App or antnode), earn ANT for storing the network's chunks, and answer to you — and a page served from the web cannot and should not reach into your machine: the browser's own wall between a hosted page and localhost is a feature this farm respects rather than tunnels around.

⚡ OUR WALLET, YOUR TREZOR — the signing wing · founder-ruled, no MetaMask

The ruling, executed: this estate's own cockpit talks to the Trezor through Trezor Connect — the device maker's own official bridge — with no third-party wallet in between. The page composes the transaction, the device displays and signs, the page receives only the signed bytes and broadcasts them. No key ever exists outside the hardware; this code handles signatures, never secrets.
⚠ The one disclosed dependency of its kind in the estate: pressing the button below loads Trezor's official Connect script from connect.trezor.io — on your gesture only, never at page load. A signing surface that hid its bridge would be a wallet asking for blind trust; this one names it before you press anything.
🔓 Release the device first: one Trezor, one app at a time — if Trezor Suite or the Autonomi App is open, the bridge will report "used by another application" and the takeover often fails. Close them, then connect. (The founder's first bench run paid for this sentence.)
