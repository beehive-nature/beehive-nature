⛏ NODE OPERATIONS — where the farm actually runs

Your nodes run on your machine (the Autonomi App or antnode), earn ANT for storing the network's chunks, and answer to you — and a page served from the web cannot and should not reach into your machine: the browser's own wall between a hosted page and localhost is a feature this farm respects rather than tunnels around.
⚠ DESIGN · the local dock — what lands here as the sprint's T1/T2 receipts close: opened locally (the estate's own dev server beside your daemon), this panel reads your antd's local API — node count, stored chunks, earnings in attos — with nothing leaving your machine. Until those receipts land this panel stays honestly empty rather than mocked. Run the App meanwhile: ant-ui releases (v0.9.7+ carries the batch-payment fixes) · node lane: ant-node.

THE FARM'S LAW — why one window, two chains

The farm feeds the archive; the DAO steers the names. ANT pays for the bytes our receipts and mirrors live on; Vaulta carries the identity registry those receipts cite. One treasury without the other is half an organism — so the window shows both, reads both keylessly, and holds neither. Every number above prints its method beneath it; a failed fetch renders as a failure, never as zero.
