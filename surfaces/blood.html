why a fractal, not a census
The doubling is the shape of the desire — every blood relative, for millennia. Real pedigrees collapse: at ten generations the 1,023 slots are fewer real people, because ancestors repeat. The collapse is not an embarrassment — it is exactly why the evidence layers exist: a repeated ancestor is a claim to verify, not a node to double-count.

the living stay roles until they consent. Named never Living — dates and places default private, DNA and health never default public. The fractal renders positions, not identities.

the evidence layers
⧗
era ≠ support
when a claim was made and what backs it are two different axes — an old claim with a weak source is old, not strong; they never merge into one score.
✶
mythology ≠ tradition
Seiðr and family lore are rendered as holder-attributed interpretation — attribution is never verification; a story stays a story until a source says otherwise.
⎔
reconstructions are versioned
a new edition of the tree never erases the last — every revision stays reachable, superseded ≠ deleted.
⚘
the living are roles until they consent
named never Living — a grandparent slot renders as a role, not an identity, until the person consents; DNA and health data never default public.
⌗
source counts = inventory · citations = confidence
how many records exist and how many claims they support are two numbers, reported separately, never averaged into one badge.
