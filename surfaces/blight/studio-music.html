bLighT Studio // music creation MVP

A 16-step sequencer that speaks the ERC-20i family's language: import any piece's on-chain scale, compose, loop live, export the score as an inscription-ready payload. Console tiers (Beginner/ Experienced/Pro, gated by your holdings) come with the booth — this MVP ships everything open with that noted.
