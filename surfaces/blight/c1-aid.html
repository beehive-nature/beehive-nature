Two numbers, both true: the uniform bound (k²/2N, the spec §4 figure) and the weighted figure (Σp² per axis — absence gates concentrate mass: two cloths "collide" most often by both being absent). For PEPi at 167 the uniform figure is 3.5×10⁻⁶ but the weighted figure is ≈ — — still comfortably rare, and the gap is the lesson: size BNRi on the weighted number.

What C-1 rules, all in one place

1 · The taxonomy itself — layers and variants per layer (the sliders above).
2 · The colour axis — HD weights vs uniform array; it changes the math materially.
3 · The level count, which is currently contradictory in the tree: CONSENT-1 D-8 promises 8 levels; the reference implementation ships levelsCount = 6 with thresholds 11/22/33/44/56 (SPEC-ERC20I-MECHANICS-1 §2). C-1 should rule the count so the encoder, the gate thresholds and the consent text agree — the levels flag is part of this ruling, not separate from it.
4 · Implicitly, C-3 — grid 48 vs 96 halves every byte figure; one toggle, shown above.
