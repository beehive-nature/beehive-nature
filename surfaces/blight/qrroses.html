      The estate's name made literal: BNRoSe's roses, Beehive Nature's bees — over a code that never moves. The plate is soil: stems root at its edge and climb outward and upward, blooms open late, bees arrive after the roses and may cross the code — ECC H has budget, and every bee path is decoder-gated frame by frame. The modules, the three finder squares and the quiet zone are frozen in every single frame: a camera samples one frame, and every frame must scan. The blooms are ROSES — layered spiralling petals in --rose, a token minted for this garden: love given to the living. ODD NUMBERS ONLY — stems, leaves, petal layers: in Latvia an even-numbered bouquet is for the grave; 3, 5, 7, 11 are for the living, and the generator cannot produce an even count (audited across seeds by the gate). The founder's wife is Latvian; Riga's flower shops stay open all night for exactly this.

      Provenance. Engine: QR-Code-generator by Project Nayuki (MIT), vendored whole — the same pin the QR TREE carries. The plate is drawn once to a frozen buffer and blitted identically every frame; roses never enter the quiet zone + 2 module widths; bees are solid and small and their paths are proven, not assumed — jsQR AND zxing read every sampled frame back to the exact URL, and a path whose frames fail is moved, never softened. Reduced motion yields one still frame that decodes. One file, zero calls.
