      the whole organ

      bLighT Organ // the seed becomes sound

      The music-inscription modality, given wind. Traits are read live from the MiDi contracts — deployed May 2024, heard for minutes, silent since June 3. The seed is the score: it picks the key, walks the scale, sets the tempo. Same seed, same song, forever. Credit to the fallen anon artist.
      pick a deploy and press read — the chain still answers her, two years silent.
      Tinker: the number is the composition. Change one digit and the song changes — the key, the melody walk, the bar colors, all of it derives from that one number. Roll the dice for a song you've never heard; when you find one you love, copy the song-link — that number is the record.
      Rendering: scale name → interval set (13 scales mapped); seed % 12 picks the key; seed bits walk the scale; duration → note length; barColorTop/Bottom paint the bars. This is the calibration bench for the encoder to come — every seed here is a candidate inscription.
