The renderer is frozen and the freeze is enforced. surfaces/forge/orbit.html, 8,644 bytes, committed-blob sha256 6e54b80d…8a6b29 , pinned from commit c3e4997. e2e/forge-freeze.mjs hashes the committed blob — never a worktree copy — and CI fails on any edit. Tinkering forks to orbit-v2.html.
Same tuple, same picture, forever. That is what the pin buys: a seed-link is a complete record of a work, because the program it runs cannot drift. Reproduce any piece by opening its link — you are running the identical bytes.
Not yet mirrored off this estate. The Arweave copy of the renderer and manifest is the ceremony's ready state, not a completed mirror — it waits on a funded JWK, which does not exist on the dev box. Until then the freeze is enforced by CI and by this repository, and nowhere else.
