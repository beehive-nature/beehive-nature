🔬 how to read a lab report

A lab report is a photograph of one sample, taken by one method, at one sensitivity.

Practice example

The report says: below the detection limit.

Does that mean there is none?

Below the detection limit means this test cannot reliably detect the amount. It does not prove that the amount is zero.

What else should I check?

Check what was measured, which sample was tested, and how sensitive the method was.
