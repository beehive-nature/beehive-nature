identity — the BNR lane's key

This identity is a nostr keypair generated on-device. It never derives from a phone number and never touches the SMS lane — the handset is not a root-key holder (E5 law).

where the Android surface comes from

GrapheneOS Messages is open source (MIT) and already carries the Android ergonomics we would otherwise build — conversation lists, notifications, camera/media sharing, ACTION_SEND intents, contact integration, default-SMS machinery. We reuse that plumbing around a separate BNR envelope, identity, encryption and storage layer; we do not inherit its trust model, and SMS stays a visibly separate low-trust lane.
