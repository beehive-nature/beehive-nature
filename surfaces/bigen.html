EXHIBIT 001 · EVIDENCE MAP — cannabis & cancer

this corpus is mechanistic + early-phase: nothing in it is poolable, so no pooled estimate will ever render on this page. a pooled number on an evidence map is a category error — the exact error this object type exists to prevent (BRIEF-02 §2 negative control). where a published pooled analysis exists (Zhang 2015, lung cancer), it is cited as their computation, never rendered as ours.

EXHIBIT 002 · EVIDENCE MAP — the COA panel undercount: a total wearing a panel's name

the founder's catch, documented from primary regulation: regulators require 4–16 analytes against ~160 cannabinoids identified in the plant — a COA's "Total Cannabinoids" line is a panel floor wearing a total's name. this corpus is regulatory tables + same-sample assays: not poolable — no pooled estimate will ever render here. source of record: RECEIPT_CANNABINOID_PANEL_UNDERCOUNT (Seat 3, 37a432f; Canada IHR figure corrected same-day at 72661ef in the sibling receipt).
claimverdictsources
the display law this exhibit exists to carry: the surfaces never render "total cannabinoids" without naming the panel — total (n of ~160 known) — three laws sharing one grammar: unmeasured ≠ zero · saturated fat ≠ one number · total ≠ panel sum.
