0 · WHY A COMPOSER — the finding that governs this build

IQ.wiki has no write API. The official MCP server exposes five read-only tools; the public API is marketed as upcoming; the only documented intake is a human typing into the Sophia chat (the "Suggest Wiki" button). Sophia is real and is the gate: she gathers the proposal, runs it against the editorial standards, and routes it to the human editorial team — most decisions within days. Outside people do not edit; they suggest. So the machine's job ends at a well-formed, well-cited, neutral-toned draft — the human is the gate no automation replaces (the same boundary the whole project rests on). 4 · THE TONE CHECK — third person, no superlatives, no advertisement

the real risk is not authorship (IQ.wiki has no COI rule) — it is tone: promotional tone is their definition of spam, judged by a human editor with no obligation to explain. the checker flags first person, second person, superlatives, promotional verbs, unfalsifiable phrasing and exclamation marks. it shows you the flagged sentence and why; it never silently rewrites. a flagged sentence can still go in — but only by your explicit keep-anyway tick, so the human decision stays visible at the seam it belongs.
