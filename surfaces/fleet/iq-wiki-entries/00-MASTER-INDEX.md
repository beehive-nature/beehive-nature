## Purpose

This document is the master index for a series of blockchain-ready encyclopedia entries documenting the **complete taxonomy of product streams derivable from industrial hemp biomass** (*Cannabis sativa L.*, ≤0.3% THC). It serves as the foundational knowledge layer for:

- **iq.wiki** — permanent, blockchain-verified reference articles
- **beehivebiomass.com** — industrial marketplace product categorization
- **Intercontinental Commodity Index** — product stream weighting & pricing methodology
- **bnature.social** — consumer education content sourcing
- **Canvas LM** — comprehension verification curriculum

## Taxonomy Structure

```
Hemp Biomass (Cannabis sativa L.)
├── 1. Cannabinoid Extracts (from flower/trim)
│   ├── CBD Isolate
│   ├── Full-Spectrum Oil
│   ├── Broad-Spectrum Oil
│   ├── Distillate
│   ├── Minor Cannabinoids (CBG, CBN, CBC, THCV)
│   └── Novel Cannabinoids (Delta-8, HHC, THC-O)
├── 2. Fiber Products (from bast fiber of stalk)
│   ├── Textiles & Fabrics
│   ├── Biocomposites
│   ├── Non-Woven Materials
│   ├── Rope & Cordage
│   └── Insulation
├── 3. Hurd / Shiv Products (from woody core of stalk)
│   ├── Hempcrete (Construction)
│   ├── Animal Bedding
│   ├── Absorbents (Spill Cleanup)
│   ├── Particle Board
│   └── Mulch & Soil Amendment
├── 4. Seed & Nutrition (from hemp seeds)
│   ├── Hemp Hearts (Dehulled Seed)
│   ├── Hemp Protein Powder
│   ├── Hemp Seed Oil
│   └── Animal Feed
├── 5. Cellulose & Bio-Materials (from whole stalk)
│   ├── Bioplastics
│   ├── Paper & Packaging
│   └── Nanocellulose
├── 6. Terpenes & Essential Oils (from flower/leaves)
│   ├── Aromatherapy Oils
│   ├── Flavorings
│   └── Fragrances
├── 7. Energy & Biochar (from whole plant)
│   ├── Biodiesel
│   ├── Bioethanol
│   ├── Biochar
│   └── Biomass Pellets
├── 8. HempSweet™ / Biochemicals (from hurd xylan)
│   ├── Xylitol (food-grade sweetener)
│   ├── Xylitol (pharma-grade)
│   ├── Furfural (chemical feedstock)
│   └── Acetic Acid (from autohydrolysis)
└── 9. Wellness & Cosmetics (from extracts + seed oil)
    ├── Topicals & Salves
    ├── Skincare Products
    ├── Nutraceuticals
    └── Tinctures
```

## Citation Standard

All entries follow the **BiGen Evidence Framework** (Cochrane Next-Generation methodology adapted for agricultural/industrial applications):

- **[A]** — Peer-reviewed research / clinical evidence
- **[B]** — Industry data / market reports
- **[C]** — Regulatory documentation
- **[D]** — Practitioner consensus / field experience

Each claim is tagged with its evidence level. Entries are designed for iq.wiki blockchain verification.
