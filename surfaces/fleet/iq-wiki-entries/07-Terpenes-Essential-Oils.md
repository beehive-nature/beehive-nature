## Summary

Terpenes are the aromatic compounds responsible for the characteristic scent and flavor of hemp and cannabis. Over 200 distinct terpenes have been identified in *Cannabis sativa*. Beyond their sensory properties, terpenes modulate cannabinoid activity through the "entourage effect" and possess independent therapeutic properties. Hemp terpenes are extracted and concentrated for use in aromatherapy, food flavoring, fragrance, and wellness formulations.

## 2. Processing Methods

### 2.1 Steam Distillation
| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Mechanism** | Steam carries volatile terpenes through condensation |
| **Yield** | 0.5–2% of biomass weight (low but pure) |
| **Quality** | Excellent — produces true essential oil |
| **Limitation** | Heat-sensitive terpenes may degrade; not ideal for all profiles |

### 2.2 CO₂ Terpene Extraction
| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Mechanism** | Subcritical CO₂ selectively extracts terpenes without heat |
| **Yield** | Higher than steam distillation |
| **Quality** | Superior — preserves heat-sensitive terpenes |
| **Advantage** | Co-extracts with cannabinoids, then separated via distillation |

### 2.3 Hydrocarbon Extraction (Terpene-Rich)
| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Mechanism** | Butane/propane at low temps preserves full terpene profile |
| **Product** | "Sauce" or high-terpene full-spectrum extract (HTFSE) |
| **Terpene content** | 30–50%+ |

## 3. Product Streams

### 3.1 Essential Oils (Aromatherapy)
| Spec | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Product** | Concentrated terpene oil for diffusion, topical (diluted), massage |
| **Profile** | Full natural terpene spectrum from hemp flower |
| **Applications** | Aromatherapy, wellness, spa, sleep support |
| **Market** | Growing with natural wellness trend |

### 3.2 Flavorings (Food & Beverage)
| Spec | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Product** | Food-grade terpene isolates or blends |
| **Applications** | Craft beverages, culinary, cannabis-infused foods |
| **Regulatory** | Generally Recognized As Safe (GRAS) for many terpenes [C] |
| **Advantage** | Natural flavoring vs synthetic alternatives |

### 3.3 Fragrances (Perfumery)
| Spec | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Product** | Terpene isolates for fragrance formulation |
| **Key terpenes** | Limonene (citrus), pinene (woody), linalool (floral) |
| **Market** | Natural fragrance movement |

### 3.4 Terpene Blends (Cannabis Product Enhancement)
| Spec | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Product** | Profile-matched terpene blends for reintroduction into distillates |
| **Purpose** | Restore terpene profile lost during distillation |
| **Advantage** | Customizable strain-specific profiles |
| **Market** | Cannabis product manufacturers |
