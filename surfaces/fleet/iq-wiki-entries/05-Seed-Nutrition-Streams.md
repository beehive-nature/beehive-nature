## Summary

Hemp seeds are one of the most nutritionally complete plant foods on Earth, containing all nine essential amino acids, an optimal omega-3 to omega-6 fatty acid ratio, and digestible protein at concentrations exceeding most other seeds. Hemp seed products — including dehulled hearts, protein powder, and cold-pressed oil — represent a rapidly growing segment of the global functional foods market. Unlike cannabinoid products, hemp seed products contain negligible cannabinoids and are legally classified as food in most jurisdictions.

## 2. Processing

### 2.1 Dehulling (Removing the Shell)
- **Purpose:** Remove the hard outer shell to produce "hemp hearts"
- **Equipment:** Mechanical dehuller (impact or abrasive type)
- **Yield:** 55–65% hearts, 35–45% shell (shell = fiber product/animal feed)
- **Quality factor:** Minimizing broken kernels

### 2.2 Cold Pressing (Oil Extraction)
- **Method:** Mechanical screw press at low temperature (<40°C)
- **Yield:** 75–90% of available oil
- **Critical:** Must stay below 40°C to preserve essential fatty acids
- **Byproduct:** Press cake (high protein — feeds protein powder production)

### 2.3 Protein Extraction
- **Source:** Press cake (post-oil extraction)
- **Process:** Milling → sieving → (optional) protein isolation
- **Protein concentration:** 50% (flour) to 70%+ (concentrate) to 90%+ (isolate)

## 3. Product Streams

### 3.1 Hemp Hearts (Dehulled Seeds)
| Spec | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Form** | Soft, nutty-tasting inner seed kernel |
| **Nutrition** | 10g protein, 14g fat (ω3+ω6), 3g fiber per 30g serving |
| **Applications** | Direct consumption, smoothies, salads, baking, granola |
| **Market** | Functional foods, superfoods, plant-based nutrition |
| **Typical retail** | $8–15/lb |
| **Key advantage** | Complete protein + allergen-friendly (no major allergens) |

### 3.2 Hemp Protein Powder
| Spec | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Form** | Fine powder, greenish-tan color |
| **Types** | Flour (50%), Concentrate (70%), Isolate (90%+) |
| **Nutrition** | 15g protein per 30g (flour) to 27g (isolate) |
| **Advantage** | Complete amino acids; highly digestible (edestin + albumin) |
| **Applications** | Protein supplements, meal replacements, plant-based foods |
| **Typical retail** | $12–20/lb (flour); $20–40/lb (isolate) |

### 3.3 Hemp Seed Oil
| Spec | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Form** | Cold-pressed liquid oil, greenish-gold |
| **Fatty acid profile** | ω6 (linoleic) ~55%; ω3 (α-linolenic) ~20%; GLA ~4% |
| **Critical property** | Perfect ω3:ω6 ratio (~1:3) — considered optimal for human health |
| **Smoke point** | 165°C (330°F) — NOT for high-heat cooking; use as finishing oil |
| **Shelf life** | 6–12 months refrigerated; oxidizes rapidly if exposed to heat/light |
| **Applications** | Culinary (salads, dips, smoothies), supplements, cosmetics |
| **Typical retail** | $15–30/16oz |

### 3.4 Animal Feed
| Spec | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Source** | Whole seed, screening, or press cake |
| **Applications** | Poultry, cattle, aquaculture, pet food |
| **Advantage** | High protein, omega-rich — improves egg/meat fatty acid profiles |
| **Regulatory status** | Varies by jurisdiction; approved for some species in some regions [C] |

## 4. Quality Standards

| Grade | Product | Key Metrics |
|---|---|---|
| **S1-Food** | Food-grade seed/hearts | >99% purity, <9% moisture, viable, certified |

| **S2-Feed** | Feed-grade | Variable purity, for animal consumption only |
