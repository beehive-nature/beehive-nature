## Summary

Hemp hurd can be compressed with bio-based binders to produce structural lumber, flooring, and engineered wood panels that rival hardwood in density, durability, and workability. Hemp lumber is ready to harvest in 120 days versus 20-60 years for oak, walnut, or maple. Commercial pioneers like HempWood (Kentucky, USA) have proven the concept commercially.

## 1. The Product

| Property | Hemp Lumber | Oak (comparison) |
|---|---|---|
| **Density** | 700-900 kg/m3 | 670-770 kg/m3 |
| **Janka hardness** | ~2,000 lbf | 1,290-2,160 lbf (white oak) |
| **Growth time** | **120 days** | 20-60 years |
| **Workability** | Cuts, sands, finishes like wood | Standard |
| **Carbon footprint** | **Carbon-negative** | Carbon-neutral (slow) |

---

## 2. Processing

**Production Method:** Hemp Hurd -> Drying (<8% moisture) -> Sizing -> Mixing with bio-resin -> Hot Pressing (150-200C, 5-15 MPa) -> Cooling -> Milling/Sanding -> Finished Hemp Lumber

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| **Hurd moisture** | <8% before pressing |
| **Binder** | Soy-based, PLA, or bio-epoxy (petroleum-free options available) |
| **Press temperature** | 150-200C |
| **Press pressure** | 5-15 MPa |
| **Press time** | 5-15 minutes per cycle |
