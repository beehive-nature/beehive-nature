## Summary

Wellness and cosmetics represent the consumer-facing endpoint of hemp product streams, combining cannabinoid extracts (Entry 02), terpenes (Entry 07), and hemp seed oil (Entry 05) into finished products including topicals, skincare formulations, nutraceuticals, and tinctures. This is the largest consumer market segment by product count and the primary revenue driver for the bnature.social commerce platform.

## 2. Product Streams

### 2.1 Topicals & Salves
| Spec | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Base** | Hemp seed oil or coconut oil + beeswax |
| **Active** | CBD/CBG (50–1000 mg per container) |
| **Applications** | Muscle/joint relief, skin conditions, localized application |
| **Bioavailability** | Topical — no systemic absorption (non-psychoactive) |
| **Market** | Largest CBD wellness category |
| **Typical retail** | $25–75 per container |

### 2.2 Skincare Products
| Spec | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Types** | Serums, creams, cleansers, masks, lip balms |
| **Active ingredients** | Hemp seed oil (EFA), CBD (anti-inflammatory), terpenes |
| **Properties** | Anti-inflammatory, moisturizing, sebum-regulating, antioxidant |
| **Skin benefits** | Acne reduction, anti-aging, barrier repair |
| **Market** | Natural/clean beauty segment — growing 10–15% CAGR |
| **Typical retail** | $20–80 per product |

### 2.3 Tinctures (Sublingual Oils)
| Spec | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Base** | Hemp seed oil, MCT oil, or olive oil |
| **Active** | CBD/CBG/CBN isolate or full-spectrum (250–5000 mg) |
| **Delivery** | Sublingual (under tongue) for systemic absorption |
| **Onset** | 15–45 minutes |
| **Duration** | 4–6 hours |
| **Bioavailability** | 20–35% (higher than edibles) |
| **Typical retail** | $30–120 per bottle |

### 2.4 Nutraceuticals
| Spec | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Types** | Capsules, softgels, gummies, lozenges |
| **Active** | CBD/CBG isolate or broad-spectrum |
| **Advantage** | Precise dosing, consistent |
| **Delivery** | Oral — lower bioavailability (6–15%) but longer duration |
| **Typical retail** | $30–80 per bottle |

### 2.5 Emerging: Minor Cannabinoid Products
| Product | Active | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **CBG focus products** | CBG isolate | Focus, inflammation |
| **CBN sleep products** | CBN isolate | Sleep support |
| **Entourage blends** | Multi-cannabinoid + terpene | Full-spectrum wellness |
